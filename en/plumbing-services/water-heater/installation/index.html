When it is time to fit a new water heater

  • The tank itself leaks, or there are rust streaks underneath, around the flange.
  • The old model no longer has a heating element or thermostat, and the repair would cost almost as much as a new one.
  • A bathroom or kitchen is being redone, and the water heater needs a different place.
  • The building's hot water comes from a heat substation, but you want a reserve for the times when it is switched off.
  • The family runs short of hot water, and needs a larger volume or a second heater in the kitchen.

How a water heater is installed, step by step

  1. Place and wall A wall that holds a full tank is chosen: brick, concrete or, in a wooden house, a log or a stud. A water heater is not hung on plasterboard lining; a fixing that reaches the load-bearing wall or a frame is needed.
  2. Shutting off the water and draining the old water heater First the cold water is shut off and the hot tap is opened, so that air enters the tank as it empties and no vacuum forms. Then the water is drained and the old water heater is taken down.
  3. Fixings Holes are drilled and anchors suited to this wall are fitted, and the water heater is hung level.
  4. Safety valve on the cold supply The valve is fitted directly on the cold water supply, with no shut-off valve between it and the water heater. Its opening pressure must match the water heater's maximum pressure, which the manufacturer states for the particular model.
  5. Drain and pressure The valve's drain is run open downwards to a trap or the drains. If the mains pressure is too high, a pressure reducer is fitted before the water heater.
  6. Filling before electricity With the hot tap open, the tank is filled until the air comes out and the stream becomes steady; then every joint is checked. Only after that is the water heater switched on, because in an empty tank the heating element overheats.

The manufacturer Dražice specifies a safety valve for every pressurised water heater, no shut-off valve between the water heater and the valve, and a pressure reducer if the mains pressure is above 5.5 bar when nobody is drawing water. Ariston specifies the drain pipe open to the air and pointing down. Checked 13.11.2026.

Replacing a water heater when the old one hangs on old screws

The fixing holes of a new water heater rarely match the old ones, and old wall plugs are often crumbled. So a replacement is drilled afresh, and the plumber checks that the wall behind the tile is not loose. The old supply pipes and safety valve may also be clogged with limescale: it can be worth replacing them together with the water heater. Taking down the old water heater is a separate job.

Connecting an electric water heater: the socket and protection

Wall-hung water heaters are usually connected to a socket or a dedicated line. A bathroom and a kitchen are the least safe rooms as far as electricity goes, so check with an electrician that the circuit has residual-current protection. Latvian Building Code LBN 261-15 has required, since 01.07.2025, residual-current protection with a tripping current of no more than 30 mA for general-purpose sockets up to 32 A; how this applies to an old installation, ask an electrician.

Installing an instantaneous water heater under the sink

An instantaneous heater has no tank: it heats the water only while the tap is open. It suits a kitchen sink or a small hand basin where there is no room for a water heater. It needs a high-power electrical connection, so first an electrician says whether the line will cope. The plumber connects the heater to the water supply and the sink tap.

The neighbour below and a wooden partition: where the risk is

In a block of flats, a loose joint or a blocked drain means water at the neighbour below, so the supply pipes are fitted so they can be shut off without opening a box. In an old wooden house in Āgenskalns or Torņakalns the weakest point is the wooden partition: if it is plaster on laths, a heavy tank pulls the fixings out over time. If the partition has no log or stud to fix to, a smaller water heater or another place is better.

What people usually ask

Can a water heater be hung above the toilet in a Soviet-era bathroom?

Often that is the only free place. What matters is that the wall behind the toilet is load-bearing or the fixing reaches it, and that the supply pipes and the valve can be reached.

Why must no tap be fitted between the water heater and the safety valve?

If the tap were closed, the valve could not release the expansion water, and the pressure in the tank would rise. The manufacturer Dražice expressly forbids it.

Where should the water from the safety valve be drained?

Through a pipe downwards to a trap or the drains, with an open end. It must not be plugged, because the valve usually drips during heating.

Is the electrical connection included in the price?

No. Water heater installation from €70 includes fixing and the water connection; the electrical connection is a separate job.
