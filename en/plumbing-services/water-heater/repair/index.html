If the water heater does not heat or trips the fuse, the fault is usually in the electrical part; if it drips, you have to look at where from. If the tank itself leaks, repair no longer pays off, and the water heater is replaced.

Signs that point to the fault

  • The water stays cold although the light is on: usually the heating element.
  • The light is off and the water does not warm: the thermostat, its safety cut-out or the connection.
  • It trips the residual-current switch when switched on: the heating element may be leaking.
  • The safety valve keeps dripping when the water heater is not heating: the pressure or the valve itself.
  • It drips under the flange or along the bottom of the tank: the gasket or the tank.
  • The hot water smells or comes with white grains: limescale and the anode.

The water heater will not heat or trips the fuse

In an electric water heater the water is heated by the heating element, which is switched on and off by the thermostat. The thermostat usually also has a safety cut-out that switches heating off in case of overheating, and it does not switch back on by itself. If the heating element is coated with a thick layer of limescale, it overheats and burns out.

If the water heater trips the residual-current switch, the fault is often in the casing of the heating element, where water has got in. Do not switch such a water heater on again until it has been inspected: a bathroom is a damp room, and an electrical leak there is dangerous. To keep limescale from damaging the element, the Ariston manual provides for descaling it about every 2 years, and sooner in hard water.

The water heater leaks: from the valve, the flange or the tank

Water expands as it heats, so the safety valve usually drips during heating, and that is normal. The Dražice manual states that fully heated water takes up about 3.5% more space than cold water. If the valve keeps dripping even when the water heater is switched off and nobody is drawing water, the cause is usually mains pressure above the valve's limit, or a worn valve spring and seal. Then the valve is replaced, or a pressure reducer is fitted before the water heater.

If it drips around the flange at the bottom of the tank, the gasket has usually worn out, and it is replaced. If water comes through the tank wall or seam, the tank has rusted. The magnesium anode protects the tank from corrosion; Dražice and Ariston advise checking or replacing it after 2 years. A rusted tank is not repaired, so see water heater installation.

Dražice OKCE/OKHE manual: dripping during heating, the valve must not drip without heating, anode after 2 years; Ariston Velis Evo manual: descaling the heating element about every 2 years. Checked 13.11.2026.

How the repair goes

  1. Disconnect and cool The water heater is disconnected from the electricity and the water is left to cool.
  2. Drain The cold water is shut off, the hot tap is opened so air enters the tank, and the water is drained.
  3. Find the faulty part The bottom cover is removed, the heating element and thermostat are checked with a tester, and the gasket and anode are inspected.
  4. Replace The faulty heating element, thermostat, anode, gasket or safety valve is replaced; if the tank has a lot of limescale, it is removed on the same visit.
  5. Refill and test The tank is filled before switching on, the flange and joints are checked, then it is switched on and watched to see that it heats.

The repair price depends on the part and the model

There is no single price for water heater repair. The amount is made up of the part to be replaced and the work on the tank (whether draining it is enough or it has to be taken off the wall). It also depends on whether parts for this model are available at all, and on a comparison with the price of a new water heater. A call-out with inspection and finding the fault is from €40. When the water heater drips onto the neighbour's ceiling

In a block of flats, a water heater that drips at night can reach the neighbour below before you notice. If it drips from the tank or the flange, disconnect the water heater from the electricity, close the cold water at the water heater or at the flat's incoming supply, and warn the neighbour. If the flat's valve does not close, only the building manager can shut off the riser. If the water has already come through the ceiling, see flooded flat.

What people usually ask

Can a water heater with a leaking tank be repaired?

Usually not. A rusted tank is not repaired; replacing the water heater is then the better option.

The water heater trips the fuse. May I switch it on again?

Better not. The heating element is often leaking, and in a damp bathroom an electrical leak is dangerous. Leave the water heater disconnected until it is inspected.

The valve drips every night. Is that a fault?

If it drips while the water heater is heating, that is normal. If it also drips during hours when heating is off and the taps are closed, the pressure is usually too high or the valve is faulty.

When does a new water heater work out better than a repair?

If the tank leaks, if there are no spare parts for the model, or if several parts have to be replaced at once. On the call, compare the repair price with water heater replacement, which is from €70.
