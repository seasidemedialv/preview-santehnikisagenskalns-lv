In a flat: up to the riser it is yours, the riser is the building's

Under the Apartment Ownership Law, the flat includes the utilities up to the building's risers and the sanitary fittings: toilet, shower, bath. Jobs such as replacing a tap, a toilet, a trap or pipes you can usually order yourself. The riser and a radiator that depends on the building's heating are common property: then talk to the building manager first, because the riser is shut off by their people.

The Residential Property Management Law puts the supply of water and drainage, and the maintenance and routine repair of the building's utilities, on the building manager. So if the leak is in the riser or the shared horizontal drain in the basement is blocked, the first call is to the building manager; you may need a plumber in the flat after the riser has been shut off.

Apartment Ownership Law, Sections 3, 4 and 11; Residential Property Management Law, Section 6. Checked on 16.11.2026.

In a wooden house and a private house: the system up to the boundary of the city network

The Rīgas ūdens network ends at the outer wall of the meter chamber or the drain inspection chamber. Everything on the house side is usually the owner's responsibility: the incoming supply pipe, the yard sewer line, the water heater and the heating. In an old wooden house in Āgenskalns or Torņakalns it often has to be looked at as a whole, so it is worth reading plumbing in an old wooden house.
