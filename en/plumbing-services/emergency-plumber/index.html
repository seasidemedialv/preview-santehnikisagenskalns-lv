Which valve to close: in the flat, in a wooden house and in the basement

In a block of flats, water reaches each floor from risers that run through the whole building. Your flat's pipes branch off the riser, and they usually have two valves, one for cold and one for hot water, often next to the meters in the bathroom or toilet. In Soviet-era series blocks they are often behind a hatch in the riser box. If you do not know which pipe is leaking, close both.

If the leak is before your valve, for example the riser itself or its joint with the branch pipe, the flat's valve does not help. Then the riser has to be shut off in the basement. It is the building's common property, and shutting it off is organised by the building manager, so it is worth finding their emergency contact in the building's notices now, not when the water is running.

In an old wooden house in Āgenskalns, Torņakalns, Ķīpsala or Zasulauks, the incoming supply pipe enters from the street into the basement or under the floor, and the main valve is behind the water meter, sometimes in a meter pit in the yard. If the house has several flats, this valve stops the water for all of them, so tell the neighbours. If a meter seal has to be removed in an emergency, Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17 allow it, but Rīgas ūdens must be notified within three working days at the latest.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 4: the building's utility systems are common property. Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17, clause 64: in an emergency the user may remove the meter seal, notifying Rīgas ūdens within three working days at the latest. Checked 16.11.2026.

A water leak: is the pipe just dripping or has it burst?

A dripping joint gives you time: you can put a bowl under it and close the valve calmly. A burst pipe under pressure sprays, and the water quickly reaches the neighbour below. Then the valve comes first, belongings after.

In old buildings galvanised steel pipe rusts over time. The US water utility EBMUD explains that once the zinc wears away, rust builds up inside and narrows the pipe: the pressure falls and the water turns brownish. In a wooden house, the second cause is frost. Ice is about 9% less dense than water, so it expands, and for a pipe by a cold outer wall or in an unheated basement that ends in a crack. A frozen pipe is thawed slowly, with the valve closed and a tap open, using a warm cloth or a hot-water bottle, never an open flame.

Corrosion of galvanised steel pipes: East Bay Municipal Utility District (USA). Density of ice: US Geological Survey (USGS). Thawing without an open flame: British water industry advice (Affinity Water, WaterSafe). Checked 13.11.2026.

Finding a leak when the stain is on the ceiling but the taps are dry

A hidden leak in a wall or floor is usually given away by its effects: bubbling or peeling paint, yellow or brown patches, a smell of mould or a quiet sound of running water. In a flat, first close all taps and look at the water meters. If the reading keeps changing, water is escaping somewhere in your pipes beyond the meter.

If the meters stand still but the stain grows, the water may come from above: from the neighbour's bath, from a riser or from a drain joint that leaks only when someone uses water. Then the first step is to go up a floor. If the water has already reached your neighbour's flat, what to do next is written on the page flooded flat.

In a private or wooden house with its own incoming supply, you can also check the pressure: the British water industry's advice is to close all taps and connect a pressure gauge to the outside tap. If the pressure falls within a few hours, something leaks. If the incoming pipe in the yard is leaking, it often shows as a wet spot on the lawn, sunken paving or a lusher patch of grass while everything around is dry. Some external leaks are not noticeable.

Signs of a hidden leak and the pressure gauge check: WaterSafe (UK). Signs of a leak in an underground supply pipe: Yorkshire Water. Checked 13.11.2026.

While the plumber is on the way: electricity, the neighbour below and belongings

  • Close the flat's or the building's valve and switch off the water heater if it is on the pipe that is leaking.
  • If water is running near sockets or the fuse box, switch off the relevant circuit breaker only if you can do it without stepping into water; keep away from exposed wires.
  • Go to the neighbour below and tell them what has happened: they can move belongings and check their ceiling.
  • Lift rugs, documents and electrical appliances off the floor.
  • Photograph the place where it leaks and the traces of water while they are fresh.
  • Clear the way to the valves, the riser box and the sink cupboard, so the plumber does not have to carry belongings out.

Do not use electrical appliances that have been in water until an electrician has checked that it is not dangerous.

Electricity after flooding: Electrical Safety First advice; the State Fire and Rescue Service (VUGD) and AS Sadales tīkls advise switching off the electricity supply if the water level is rising. Checked 13.11.2026.

What people usually ask

Water is leaking through our ceiling, but the neighbour above is not at home. What should I do?

Contact the building manager: they can shut off the riser in the basement, and the water at the neighbour's will stop. The Apartment Ownership Law obliges an owner to let in specialists authorised by the manager if work is needed for the building's utilities to operate, but how this goes in a particular case is decided by the building manager. A plumber does not enter a locked flat.

May I shut off the riser in the basement myself?

Usually not. The riser is the building's common property, and its maintenance and repair are the manager's task, so it is shut off by the building manager or someone they entrust with it. The basement is often locked, and if you close the wrong riser, other floors are left without water.

In a wooden house I closed the valve, but the water still leaks. Why?

An old valve often does not close all the way, or the leak is before it, in the incoming supply pipe. Then the valve in the meter pit has to be closed. The Rīgas ūdens network usually ends at the outer wall of the meter pit or at the plot boundary, and beyond that the pipe is the owner's responsibility; if it leaks on the street side, report it to Rīgas ūdens.

If a pipe bursts on a Saturday night, will anyone respond?

Yes, we work also at night and at weekends. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, and on the call you talk through the job and the price. Until then, close the valve and warn the neighbour below.
