In an old building two questions decide the scope of the job: what wall the sink will hang on, and at what height the old outlet comes out. If both are fine, the job is short; if not, extra fixings or a new outlet come on top.

When a sink is replaced

  • The sink has a crack or a chipped corner, and water seeps into the cabinet.
  • The sink is pulling away from the wall because the wall plugs have crumbled.
  • A bathroom renovation: the new cabinet needs a different sink size.
  • A new worktop is going into the kitchen, and the old sink no longer fits it.
  • The sink is too low or too high for the people who use it.

Wall, height, trap: fitting a sink in an old building

  1. Height and position The height of the sink rim is marked, and it is checked whether the existing supply pipes and the outlet reach it. If the outlet is too low or too high, a trap with an extension can help, but not without limit.
  2. Fixing to suit the wall Different fixings suit a brick wall, a concrete panel, a plasterboard partition and a plank wall in a wooden house. A heavy sink is not hung on a light partition without a supporting cabinet or a bracket.
  3. The trap directly under the sink The trap goes right behind the fitting and must be removable or have a cleaning opening. Under LBN 221-15 the minimum waste pipe diameter is 32 mm for a hand basin and 40 mm for a kitchen sink.
  4. Water supply The flexible hoses of the mixer tap are connected to angle valves. If there are no valves or they do not close, they are replaced first.
  5. Testing The sink is filled to the overflow and emptied while watching the trap connections and the supply pipes under the sink. The joint at the wall is sealed with sanitary silicone.

Sink height: what the building code sets

For residential buildings, LBN 221-15 gives the top edge of a hand basin as usually 800 mm above the floor and the edge of a kitchen sink as 850 mm, with a tolerance of ±20 mm. A wall-mounted mixer tap is fitted 200 mm above the basin edge. These are guideline heights; if everyone in the household is tall or the children are small, the height can be adjusted, if the outlet allows it.

LBN 221-15 “Internal water supply and sewerage of buildings”, Annex 14 (installation heights of fittings) and Annex 3 (waste pipe diameters). Source: likumi.lv, checked on 13.11.2026.

Installing a kitchen sink in a worktop

Fitting a kitchen sink into a worktop starts with a cut-out made to the sink manufacturer's template. On the call, find out who does it: the plumber or the kitchen fitter. The edge of the sink is sealed so that water does not reach the edge of the chipboard, because a swollen worktop is hard to repair.

Under a kitchen sink you often need a trap with a branch for a dishwasher. This is allowed because both appliances are in the same room; LBN 221-15 forbids connecting appliances from different rooms to one trap. Rīgas ūdens advises putting fat and coffee grounds in the rubbish and using a strainer in the sink: fat settles on the pipe walls and gradually narrows the branch to the riser.

When the old outlet is not where the new sink is

Moving a sink by half a metre seems simple, but the waste pipe to the riser needs a fall. For pipes up to 50 mm, LBN 221-15 requires 30 ‰, which is 3 cm per metre. The further from the riser, the higher the outlet at the wall has to come out. In a Soviet-era panel wall, running the pipe in means chasing, and sometimes it is simpler to run the pipe above the floor behind the cabinet.

If in a wooden house in Zasulauks or Torņakalns the sink is on an outer wall and the pipe runs along it, the pipe can freeze in winter, so a new outlet is better routed along an inner wall.

What people usually ask

Is it possible to hang a sink on a plasterboard wall?

Only if a mounting frame or a wooden insert is built into the wall. Without it, the sink is supported on a cabinet or on brackets fixed to the load-bearing part.

Can a dishwasher be connected to the kitchen sink trap?

Yes, with a trap that has a branch for the machine's hose. Both appliances are in the same room, so this complies with LBN 221-15. For the connection itself, see the page on washing machine connection.

I bought the sink myself. Will you install it?

Yes, the price is for labour only; materials are extra. Check whether the set includes a trap and fixings, and enter the model in the form.
