Plumbing fittings in Āgenskalns - taps, toilets, sinks, shower

Plumbing installation in Āgenskalns, Riga, is most often the replacement of a single fitting on the existing supply pipes: a mixer tap, sink, toilet, shower cabin or washing machine connection.

In a flat, the water and drain branches of each fitting run to the riser, so before the job it matters whether your flat's valves still hold water. In a wooden house it matters more how the pipes run along the floor and the outer wall. Labour starts from €25 for replacing a mixer tap, materials extra.

From the fitting to the riser: what is yours and what is the whole building's

In a block of flats, cold and hot water come from the riser through the flat's valves, while waste water leaves along the flat's branch to the shared soil stack. Under the Apartment Ownership Law, the flat itself includes its bath, shower and toilet equipment and every utility line as far as the shared risers. So the tap, the toilet, the trap and the pipes up to the riser are usually yours, unless the building's documents say otherwise.

Replacing a fitting does not usually affect common property, so it usually does not need approval from the building manager. The situation is different if the old flat valves no longer close fully. Then a tap or a toilet cannot be replaced until the riser is shut off, and that is done by the building manager or someone they appoint. All the neighbours on that riser are without water for that time, so such work is planned in advance.

On the drain side the weakest point is the connection to the riser. In Soviet-era buildings it is often a cast-iron socket at the floor behind the toilet. If the socket is cracked, replacing the toilet becomes pipe work, and then waste pipe replacement is the page to read.

In an old wooden house in Torņakalns or Zasulauks the shut-off valve is at the building's incoming supply pipe, and there are no riser neighbours. On the other hand, the floorboards under a toilet or a bath may have sagged, and pipes along the outer wall freeze in winter.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 3 (the elements of a flat up to the common property risers) and Section 11 (work in a flat without approval, as long as it does not affect common property). Source: likumi.lv, checked on 16.11.2026. For the rules of a specific building, ask the building manager.

A tap drips even when closed: mixer tap repair or replacement

If a mixer tap drips constantly, even when closed, the usual culprit is a damaged or limescaled ceramic cartridge. It can be replaced if a cartridge is available for that model. A short drip right after closing is normal and is not a fault, as the manufacturer hansgrohe also writes.

If the tap body is cracked, there is no cartridge or the tap is very old, replacing it is cheaper. Installing or replacing a mixer tap is from €25 per item. Flexible supply hoses, if the old ones have hardened, are separate, and it is worth replacing them together with the tap.

A smell from the sink or slow water: replacing the trap

A trap holds a water seal that stops drain air from coming into the room. UK building regulations require that at least 25 mm of water remains in the trap in use. If the trap is cracked, has settled or its connection to the wall outlet is not airtight, you will smell it even when the water runs.

A new trap goes directly under the fitting, and it must be removable or have a cleaning opening. LBN 221-15 forbids connecting fittings from different rooms to one trap. A trap has no separate price: it is replaced within a call-out (from €40) or at the hourly rate (from €25/h). If the water does not go away even after cleaning, the cause may be deeper: see why water will not drain.

Clauses 213 and 216 of the Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 on water supply and drainage inside buildings; UK Approved Document H, Table 1. Checked on 13.11.2026.

The toilet leaks at the floor or the cistern keeps running

Toilet repair usually means replacing the cistern mechanism. If water runs into the bowl continuously after flushing, Geberit names the most common reason: dirt or limescale between the valve seal and the opening. Sometimes cleaning is enough, but a worn fill valve or mechanism is replaced. Cistern repair is from €35, parts extra.

Water on the floor at the base of the toilet is a more serious sign. The source may be the cistern feed, the seal where cistern meets bowl, or the outlet at the riser. The last one leaks right above the neighbour's ceiling, and the slab does not show damp for a long time. If the neighbour below already sees a stain, start with what to do if water is leaking from above.

A concealed toilet: reaching the mechanism without opening the wall

Repairing a concealed toilet looks frightening because the cistern is behind the tiles. According to Geberit, the flush and fill valves of a concealed cistern are replaced after the push panel is removed: behind it is a service opening, and the tiles stay intact. In a Sigma cistern an angle shut-off valve is built into the water supply, so the water can be shut off at the cistern itself.

Geberit does not recommend descaling the inside of the cistern: pieces of limescale that come loose can block the system. The manufacturer has extended the availability of spare parts for concealed cisterns to 50 years. Work through the button opening can cost more than on an exposed cistern, because there is little room.

Before the plumber comes: valves, the neighbour below and an empty niche

  • Find the flat's valves and try whether they close. If not, note it in the form.
  • Find the building manager's contact, in case the riser may need to be shut off.
  • If it leaks at the toilet or under the bath, warn the neighbour below so that they look at their ceiling.
  • Clear the space under the sink and the toilet niche, where the valves and meters usually are.
  • If you bought the fitting yourself, keep it and its instructions at hand; you can enter the model name in the form.
  • Attach a photo of the old connection: it shows whether adapters or new valves are needed.

What people usually ask

Will replacing a tap mean shutting off the water to the whole riser?

Only if your flat's valves do not close. If they hold water, the tap is replaced by shutting off only your own flat. If they do not, the riser is shut off by the building manager or someone they appoint, and the work is planned together with them.

Does replacing a toilet or a sink have to be approved by the building manager?

Usually not: the Apartment Ownership Law allows work inside a flat without approval, as long as it does not affect common property. If the riser has to be touched, the building manager is needed. If you are not sure how it is in your building, ask the building manager.

In a wooden house the floor by the toilet rocks. Can a new toilet be fitted?

A floor toilet needs a firm base. If the boards give way, the toilet moves, and over time the connection to the outlet starts to leak. Floor repair is another trade; it is done before the toilet is installed, or a wall-hung toilet on a frame is chosen, if there is a stable wall.

I need to replace a tap, a trap and a cistern mechanism. How do I request that?

List all the jobs in one form and attach photos. Such small jobs are usually done by the hour, from €25/h. On the call you talk through the job and the price.
