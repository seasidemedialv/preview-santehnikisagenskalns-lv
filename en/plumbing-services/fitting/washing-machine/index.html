In a small Soviet-era flat the machine often has no proper place: the bathroom lacks width, in the kitchen the outlet has to be shared with the sink, and the hallway has no connections at all. The place decides whether connecting is enough or a new outlet is needed.

Three places for a machine in a small flat

place what is already there what is usually needed
Bathroom cold water and the bath or sink outlet a tap for the machine and a trap with a branch, or a separate outlet
Kitchen the sink supply pipes and trap a trap with a branch and a tee tap on the cold water
Hallway or a niche usually nothing a new water and drain point to the riser

In the panel blocks of Bolderāja, where building began in 1965, the place for a machine is usually chosen from these three. If it will be in the bathroom, remember that Sadales tīkls calls the bathroom and the kitchen the least safe rooms as far as electricity goes, and advises pressing the T button on the residual-current device once a year to make sure it trips. The socket and the electrics are an electrician's work.

Connecting to the manufacturer's instructions

  1. Cold water Bosch requires the machine to be connected only to cold drinking water with a new inlet hose supplied in the box. The screw connection is tightened by hand.
  2. Pressure A Bosch machine needs 1–10 bar at the supply and at least 8 l/min. If the pressure in the building is higher, a pressure reducer is fitted before the machine.
  3. Drain height For the height of the drain hose from the floor to the outlet, Bosch gives 60–100 cm (0–100 cm for the AquaStop model) and Samsung 60–90 cm. The exact value is in the instructions for the specific model.
  4. Outlet to the drains The hose is connected to the trap branch or to a separate outlet. For a machine's waste pipe, LBN 221-15 sets a minimum diameter of 40 mm.
  5. Test cycle A short programme is run, and both connections are watched while the machine takes in water and pumps it out.

Connecting a dishwasher in the kitchen

A dishwasher is usually connected under the kitchen sink: a tee tap with its own valve is added to the water, and the drain hose is connected to the trap branch. For a built-in dishwasher, the hoses are first passed through the cabinet's side wall, and only then is the machine pushed into its niche. Connecting to the existing supply costs the same as for a washing machine, from €35. Where the machine takes its water and what hose height to observe is set by the instructions of the specific model.

A fridge with an ice maker: water connection

A fridge with an ice maker or a water dispenser needs a thin cold water supply, usually from a tee valve under the sink. The tube is run along the furniture so that it is not crushed when the fridge is pushed back. This is a small job, and it is done within a call-out (from €40) or at the hourly rate (from €25/h), following the fridge manufacturer's instructions.

Labour price and when it goes up

Connection to an existing tap and outlet is from €35 per machine. If a new water or drain outlet has to be built, it is from €35 per point, with chasing and materials extra. In a hallway both points are usually needed. Labour only; materials are extra. If there is a travel charge, it is stated on the call before the work.

A hose that slipped out floods the neighbour below

A leak does not always mean a faulty machine: it is enough for the drain hose to slip out of the bath or the sink. Bosch requires the hose to be secured and its end not to be submerged in the pumped-out water, otherwise the water can be sucked back into the machine. A connection to the trap or to a wall outlet reduces this risk.

The inlet hose is under pressure the whole time the tap is open. If nobody is in the flat for several days, it is worth turning off the machine's tap: in a block of flats, water from your floor goes straight to the neighbour's ceiling.

What people usually ask

Can a washing machine be connected to hot water so that it heats faster?

The Bosch instructions do not allow it: the machine is connected only to cold drinking water. For other manufacturers' requirements, see your instructions.

Can the drain hose simply be hooked over the bath?

It can, if it is secured and its end is not in the water. Still, the hose can slip out, and then the water is on the floor. A permanent connection to the trap is a smaller risk for the neighbours below.

There is no pipe in the hallway. Can the machine go there?

It can, if a water and drain point can be built to the riser with the required fall. That is a separate job from €35 per point. On the call you talk through the job and the price.
