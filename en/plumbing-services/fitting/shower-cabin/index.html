The job covers putting the cabin together, connecting the tray with its trap, connecting the water, and sealing. In an old building the deciding factor is the floor height against the outlet: it determines whether a tray fits or a shower drain can be used.

Signs your shower needs a plumber

  • The tray is cracked or gives way underfoot.
  • After a shower there is water on the floor outside the cabin.
  • The cabin door does not close or the seals have crumbled.
  • There is a smell from the drain or water stands in the tray.
  • A stain appears on the bathroom ceiling of the neighbour below.

A cabin in a prepared spot: how the work goes

  1. Checking the spot The floor level, the water supply and the drain outlet position are checked against the layout in the cabin instructions. If the outlet is too high for the tray trap, you need a taller tray or another solution.
  2. Tray and trap The tray is set on its feet or frame and levelled. The trap is connected to the outlet; Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 sets a minimum diameter of 40 mm for a shower waste pipe. According to Geberit, a shower trap is hard to clean while the tray stays in place, so access under the tray has to be left.
  3. Water The mixer tap or the cabin panel is connected to the cold and hot water, and the connections are tested under pressure.
  4. Walls and door The glass walls and door are assembled following the manufacturer's diagram and adjusted so that the door closes without a gap.
  5. Sealing and testing The joints are sealed with sanitary silicone; Sika gives its silicone a cure of about 3 mm in 24 hours. Then the shower is tested with water along all the edges.

A cracked tray: shower tray replacement

If the tray is cracked, water goes under it, and fixing the trap connection no longer helps. Replacing a shower tray means taking apart the lower part of the cabin, and the new tray has to be exactly the same size and shape to fit the cabin walls. The trap is usually replaced together with the tray, because the old one rarely fits the new outlet.

A leaking shower cabin: where to look

Shower cabin repair starts with finding the leak. If the water is on the floor outside the cabin, the cause is usually the door seals or the silicone joint. If the water comes from below, it can be the trap connection, a cracked tray or the supply to the mixer tap. The last two leak unnoticed until the neighbour below sees a stain. The repair is done within a call-out (from €40) or at the hourly rate (from €25/h), parts extra.

Shower drain installation and floor height in an old building

Installing a shower drain means a shower area without a tray, where the water runs into a floor drain. Drains under EN 1253 usually have a 50 mm water seal, and for a DN 50 drain the standard minimum flow rate is 0.8 l/s at a 20 mm water depth; the exact figures depend on the model. LBN 221-15 provides for a 50 or 100 mm waste pipe for a floor drain.

The hardest part is the fall. For a waste pipe up to 50 mm, LBN 221-15 requires 30 ‰, or 3 cm per metre, and the drain needs room under it. In Daugavgrīva, where multi-storey blocks built in the 1950s-60s dominate, as in other blocks of flats you usually cannot deepen the floor slab between storeys for a drain. So the floor in the shower area is raised, or a tray is chosen.

The price of a drain depends on its type (linear or point), on lowering or moving the outlet, and on how large a part of the floor has to be opened up and re-screeded to a slope. Waterproofing and tiling are usually separate and are another trade.

Price for a cabin and a drain

Installing a shower cabin in a prepared spot is from €125 per set. For installing a shower drain, the price is set after inspection. The price goes up with an outlet at the wrong height, moving the water outlets and removing the old cabin. Labour only; materials are extra. If there is a travel charge, it is stated on the call before the work.

What people usually ask

Can a cabin go where there is no outlet yet?

It can, but first the water and drain outlets have to be built with the required fall to the riser. That is a separate job, and the cabin price from €125 applies to a prepared spot.

Can a shower with a drain be installed without raising the floor?

Only if there is room under the floor for the drain and for the fall of the waste pipe to the riser. In a block of flats the slab is usually not deepened, so it rarely works without raising the floor.

Who does the waterproofing under the tiles?

Waterproofing and tiling are another trade. The plumber prepares the drain and the outlets, the tiler lays the waterproofing and the tiles, and then the shower equipment is installed.
