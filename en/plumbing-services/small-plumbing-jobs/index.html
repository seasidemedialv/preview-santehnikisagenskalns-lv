Jobs that can be combined in one visit

  • Replacing a mixer tap in the kitchen or bathroom (from €25 each).
  • Replacing the cistern mechanism if water runs into the toilet constantly (from €35).
  • A leak under the sink or at a joint in an accessible place (from €40).
  • Connecting a new dishwasher or washing machine, if the tap and outlet are already there (from €35).
  • Installing a sink and replacing a trap (from €40).
  • Taking apart and cleaning a slowly draining trap.

If the jobs are small and together take a few hours, it is usually better value to pay the hourly rate than for each job separately. How to calculate it in your case, you talk through on the call before the work.

How to describe several jobs in one form

  1. Each job on its own line Write the room, the appliance and what is happening: “kitchen, tap drips from the spout”, “toilet, cistern runs constantly”.
  2. If you know it, add the model The make of the tap or cistern helps to tell whether the part will be available.
  3. Say what has been bought If the new tap or dishwasher is already at home, write that; if not, you decide on the call who buys the materials.
  4. Note where the valves are Write whether the flat's water valves at the riser close. It decides whether the job can be done without the building manager.

What a plumber costs by the hour

A plumber charges from €25/h, and a call-out is usually billed for at least one hour. A plumber's call-out with arrival, inspection and finding the problem is from €40; if the plumber does the job on the spot, the call-out charge is usually included in the labour amount. The price changes with the number of jobs, access (taps behind tiles, a cistern built into the wall) and whether you have to wait while somebody shuts off the riser. Labour only; materials are extra. If there is a separate travel charge, we tell you on the call before the work.

When a small job needs the riser shut off

In Soviet-era blocks the flat's valves at the riser are often old and no longer close all the way. Then even replacing a tap is not possible until the riser is shut off, and that may be done only by the building manager or a person he allows. If you know the valves are in poor condition, arrange the riser shut-off beforehand and write in the form when it will be. A water heated towel rail connected to the hot water riser also needs the riser shut off, so it is usually not an hourly job.

How to choose a plumber for small jobs: what to ask

A good plumber is one with whom it is clear before the work what you are paying for. Ask this, whoever you choose:

  • What is the hourly rate, and what is the minimum charge for a call-out?
  • Is the call-out counted into the labour price if the work is done at once?
  • Are materials separate, and who buys them?
  • Is there a travel charge?
  • Does any of the jobs need the riser shut off, and who arranges it with the building manager?
  • What happens to the price if on site it turns out that the part is different or rusted?

A small job is not a riser replacement, replacing a bath with a shower or building a new drain outlet: those have their own price and their own preparation, and are described on separate pages, for example riser replacement in a flat. If the list has one large job and several small ones, write them all, and on the call you decide what to do in one visit.

What people usually ask

Can a tap be replaced and a dishwasher connected in one visit?

Yes, if both jobs have supply pipes and the valves close. In the form, write the two jobs on separate lines.

Do parts come within the hourly rate?

No. The hourly rate from €25/h is the labour price, materials are extra. Who buys the parts, you decide on the call.

What if the flat's valve at the riser does not close?

Then the water can be shut off only at the riser, and that is done by the building manager or a person he allows. Arrange it beforehand, otherwise the small job cannot be done.

Does the plumber also work in wooden houses?

Yes. In an old wooden house in Āgenskalns or Torņakalns the supply pipes often run along an outer wall or the floor, and in the form write where the house's incoming water valve is.
