Heated towel rail replacement - on the hot water riser or electric

Replacing a heated towel rail in a Soviet-era series block in Āgenskalns or Dzirciems, Riga usually means work on the hot water riser.

The old rail is often the bend of the riser itself, so it can be removed only when the building manager shuts off and drains the riser. If the new one is fitted with a bypass and two valves, the riser will not have to be touched next time.

When the old rail has to be replaced

  • There is rust and damp marks on the bend or at the nuts.
  • The connection to the riser drips, and the tiles under it turn brown.
  • The bathroom is being renovated, and the rail is wanted in another shape, height or on another wall.
  • The rail is painted over and clogged with limescale, and water no longer heats it evenly.
  • You want a rail that also heats when the water in the riser is not hot: an electric one.

The rail is part of the hot water riser

In small Soviet-era bathrooms the hot water riser runs through all the floors, and the towel rail is built right into it. Hot water flows through the rail to the upper floors, so if it is simply cut out and capped, the hot water stops for the whole riser.

The law counts heating elements in a flat as common property if their operation depends on the building's shared utilities, and repairing the building's utilities is the building manager's task. So a rail on the riser is usually not just the flat's own equipment: unless the building's documents say otherwise, talk to the building manager about the replacement, and the building manager shuts off the riser.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 4 (heating elements in common property) and Section 3 (the flat's utilities up to the common property risers); Residential Property Management Law, Section 6. Checked on 16.11.2026.

Replacement step by step

  1. Shutting off the riser The building manager or someone they authorise shuts off the hot water riser and lets the water out. In a private house, the hot water supply or the heating loop is shut off, if the rail is on it.
  2. Removing the old rail The nuts are unscrewed; if the rail is welded in or the threads are rusted, it is cut out of the riser.
  3. Bypass and valves A bypass, a short section of pipe between the rail's supply pipes, is fitted on the riser, and a valve on each supply pipe.
  4. Fitting the new rail The brackets are fixed to the wall, the rail is connected to the valves and levelled.
  5. Filling and testing The riser is refilled, air is let out of the rail and every connection is inspected while it warms up.

A bypass and two valves: the next change without the neighbours

A bypass lets hot water keep flowing up the riser even when the rail's valves are closed. Then the rail can be removed, painted or replaced, and the neighbours above do not even notice. Without a bypass, every next job again means shutting off the riser.

The valves go on the rail's supply pipes, not on the riser itself, so that nobody can accidentally shut off the hot water for the whole building.

An electric heated towel rail: when to leave the riser alone

An electric rail heats from the mains and has nothing to do with the riser. It is chosen if, in place of the old rail, the riser is left as a straight pipe that can be hidden in the boxing or behind a cover. It is also chosen if you want heat even when the water in the riser is not hot, or if the rail is needed in another place. The plumber fixes it to the wall, but the connection to electricity in a damp room is made by an electrician.

The price of replacing a rail and what makes it dearer

Installing or replacing a heated towel rail is from €80 if the new one is connected to the same supply pipes as the old one. In a block of flats, shutting off the riser and refilling it afterwards is charged separately. If the supply pipes have to be moved, for example when the rail is moved to another wall, pipes are from €10/m.

The price goes up with a welded-in old rail, rusted threads, a bypass and valves, and tiles that have to be opened around the riser. This is labour only; materials are extra. If there is a travel charge, it is stated on the call before the work.

If the riser pipe breaks at the rail

In old buildings the hot water riser is a steel pipe that rusts from the inside. When the rail is unscrewed, the thread on the riser can break off, and then a section of the riser has to be replaced, not just the rail. That is a separate job: riser replacement in a flat. Agree on it with the building manager before the work, so that the riser is not left shut off for longer.

When the riser is refilled, the water comes with full pressure. Warn the neighbour below and be in the bathroom while the connections are checked.

What people usually ask

May I replace a heated towel rail on the riser myself, without the building manager?

A rail on the hot water riser is usually tied to the building's common property, and the riser is shut off by the building manager. Unless the building's documents say otherwise, agree the replacement with them.

Why is the new rail cold after the replacement?

There may be air in it, a valve may be closed, or there may be no hot water circulation in the riser at that moment. First check the valves, then ask the building manager about the riser.

Can a water rail be replaced with an electric one?

It can. The riser is then kept as a straight pipe or with a bypass, and the electric rail is fixed to the wall. The electrical connection is made by an electrician.

What is the starting price for a rail on the hot water riser?

From €80 for the work on the existing supply pipes. Shutting off the riser through the building manager and materials are extra.
