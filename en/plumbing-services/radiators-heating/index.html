In a block of flats a radiator hangs on a riser that runs through all the floors, so replacing or moving a radiator can begin only when the building manager shuts off and drains the riser. In a private house in Bieriņi or a wooden house with its own boiler the system is your own, and you decide on the repair.

The riser is the whole building's, the radiator behind the valve is your matter

Most of the heat Riga needs is transmitted and distributed by AS “Rīgas siltums” (Riga's district heating company). In a building the heat arrives at the heat substation in the basement and goes from there along the risers to each floor, and the radiators in the flats are connected to these risers. The building's internal heating system is inspected, maintained and repaired by the building manager.

The law counts heating elements in a flat as common property if their operation depends on the building's shared utilities. In practice the riser up to the first valve in the flat is common property, and replacing radiators in a block of flats has to be approved by the building manager. Without a calculation you may not significantly increase a radiator's output or change the connection scheme, because that affects the neighbours on the same riser.

In the heating season the riser is full of hot water. If there are no valves of its own at the radiator, then to remove it the building manager has to shut off and drain the whole riser, and for a while the neighbours above and below are cold too. So replacing radiators in a block of flats is usually planned outside the season. Ask the building manager how it is done in your building.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 4 (heating elements in common property) and Section 10 (access for the building manager's specialists); Residential Property Management Law, Section 6; the LV portāls e-consultation on approval of radiator replacement. Checked 16.11.2026.

When the heating season starts and what it means for radiator work

Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga's municipal building manager) switches on the heating in its buildings according to the weather. For at least three days in a row the outside air does not warm above +14 °C, at one point it falls to +8 °C, and the forecast promises the same for another three days. Flat owners can agree to switch the heating on earlier or later. In the buildings of other managers the arrangements are set by their contract with the owners.

A radiator replacement is better requested from the building manager before the season, because after that every riser drained takes heat from several flats. Small jobs that do not touch the riser can also be done during heating: for example, changing a thermostatic head on an existing thermostatic valve or fixing a leak behind the radiator valve, if the valve is there.

Conditions for the start of the heating season in Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks buildings: LSM, 30.09.2025. Checked 16.11.2026.

A private house or a wooden house with its own boiler

In Bieriņi, Atgāzene, Beberbeķi or an old wooden house in Torņakalns the heating can be a closed loop with its own boiler, expansion vessel and circulation pump. Here there are no riser neighbours and no building manager. If a wooden house has several flats on one boiler, agree on the work with the other owners.

The boiler's pressure gauge for a cooled-down system usually shows between 1 and 2 bar: in the Vaillant instructions it is 1.0–2.0 bar, and Worcester Bosch tops up to 1–1.5 bar. If the pressure falls again and again, something leaks or the expansion vessel has lost its pre-charge. The boiler itself is not part of this work: the plumber works with radiators, pipes, valves and flushing.

A heating pipe drips under the windowsill: heating pipe repair

In Soviet-era series blocks the supply pipes from the riser to the radiator are short steel sections with threads, and a leak most often appears at a connection or a valve. Look at whether the water comes from the radiator side or from the riser. The radiator side, if it has its own valves, can be shut off; a leak from the riser is a matter for the building manager, and you should call them at once.

If water gets onto the floor, warn the neighbour below.

A radiator cool at the top: bleeding radiators

A cool top of a radiator while the bottom is warm usually means air. In a private house it is bled like this: the heating is switched off and the radiators are left to cool, the bleed valve is opened with a key until the hissing stops and water starts to run evenly, and the valve is closed. After bleeding the pressure in a private house system usually drops, so check the boiler gauge afterwards and, if the pressure is low, top the system up.

If it is the other way round, with a warm top and a cold bottom, the cause is usually sludge, and Worcester Bosch recommends cleaning the system rather than bleeding. What you can check yourself is in the guide radiator not heating up.

The bleeding order and the pressure drop after it: Worcester Bosch, How to bleed a radiator. Pressure 1.0–2.0 bar: Vaillant ecoTEC exclusive instructions. Checked on 16.11.2026.

Flushing the heating system in a private house, not in a block of flats

The system of a block of flats is flushed as a whole, and the building manager organises it: the radiators of one flat are not flushed separately from the building's system. In a private house's loop flushing makes sense if the radiators are cold at the bottom, the water from the bleed valve is black or the pump is noisy.

A magnet helps to decide whether to flush: in the Vaillant instructions a magnetic rod is put into a sample of the heating water, and if iron oxide (magnetite) sticks to it, the loop is flushed. Afterwards the water is protected with an inhibitor, which is added only after flushing, or a magnetic filter is fitted.

What to prepare before the plumber opens the heating loop

  • In a block of flats: ask the building manager when and who can shut off the riser, and write down their contact.
  • Warn the neighbours on the same riser that their radiators will be cold for a while.
  • Clear the space under the windowsill, take down the curtains and put towels on the floor.
  • In a private house: note what the boiler's pressure gauge shows and photograph the radiator connection.
  • If you are replacing a radiator, note the size of the old one.

What people usually ask

Can a radiator in a flat be replaced when the heating is already on?

It can, if the building manager shuts off and drains the riser. Because the neighbours are then cold too, in a block of flats the replacement is usually planned outside the season. Ask the building manager how it is done in your building.

Who shuts off the riser if the radiator is replaced on the third floor?

The riser is the building's common property, so it is shut off by the building manager or a person they allow. The owner, in turn, has to let the building manager's specialists reach the utilities in the flat.

My neighbour above has a warm radiator, mine is not. Where to look for the fault?

Usually closer: air has collected in the radiator, the valve is closed or the thermostatic head is stuck. If the whole riser is cold, that is a matter for the building manager. More in the guide radiator not heating up.

A wooden house in Torņakalns has its own boiler. Does radiator replacement have to be approved?

If the house is yours and the boiler is only yours, you decide yourselves. If the house has several flat owners with shared heating, agree with them, because draining the system affects everyone.

What does it cost to replace a radiator?

Installing or replacing a radiator is from €75, and replacing a heated towel rail from €80. Shutting off the riser through the building manager is extra, and so are materials.
