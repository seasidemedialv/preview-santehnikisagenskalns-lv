The floor and the room that leaks: what to mention in the request

  • The neighbourhood and address, and for a flat also the floor.
  • The type of building: Soviet-era series block, pre-war building, wooden house or private house.
  • What is happening and where: in the kitchen, bathroom, toilet, basement or yard.
  • Whether it is leaking now and whether the water has already reached the neighbour below.
  • If the job involves a riser or a radiator: whether the building manager knows about it.
  • For several small jobs, list them all in one request.

If it is leaking now, first turn off the shut-off valve on the incoming supply pipe of the flat or house, switch off the water heater and warn the neighbour below you. If the riser itself is leaking, also report it to the building manager.

The form also has an urgency field. If water is leaking now or there is no water in the whole flat, tick it so that the specialist knows which request to start with. For a planned job, such as replacing a water heater or a toilet, urgency is not needed.

What happens after you send it

The request is received by a specialist, who contacts you within 5 minutes. On the call they clarify what you see, and together you talk through the job and the price, including whether there is a travel charge. You agree the time of the visit right there. Starting prices are on the page plumbing prices.

What people usually ask

Why is there no phone number on the site to call?

Contact is only through the form. That way the specialist sees the neighbourhood, address and problem at once, and after you submit the form they contact you within 5 minutes.

Can I fill in the request on someone else's behalf, for example for parents in Torņakalns?

Yes. Enter the address where the work is needed and the phone number on which it is easier to talk. In the problem field of the form, mention who will be at home.

Which languages can we talk in?

Latvian, English and Russian. The site also has an English and a Russian version.
