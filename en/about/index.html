The jobs done in Pārdaugava homes

We take on plumbing in a flat up to the riser and the riser sections themselves, if the building manager allows it: leaks, pipes, blocked drains, water heaters, radiators and heated towel rails, taps, toilets, sinks and showers. In wooden houses and private houses this also means the incoming supply pipe and the yard sewer line up to the boundary of the city network. We do not do tiling or waterproofing: that is a different trade. The full list: plumbing services in Pārdaugava, Riga.

Why the prices are public

For every job the site has a starting price or says what the price depends on. It is the labour price; materials are extra. Before the work, on the call you talk through the job and the price: which rows apply to your case, what can raise the total and whether there is a travel charge. All the rows: plumbing prices.

The riser, the building manager and your flat

In a block of flats we work with what belongs to the flat and with the riser section on your floor, once the building has agreed to the replacement. The riser is shut off and turned on again by the building manager or a person authorised by them. You agree the time with the building manager, and the plumber takes it into account. That way the work does not start with an argument about who may turn off the building's valve in the basement.

Why only a form

A request comes through the form (it is at the top of every page and on the page Contact), so that the specialist sees the neighbourhood, address and problem even before the call. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. In the form you can also write what is hard to explain by phone: which floor, which pipe, whether the building manager already knows. We speak Latvian, English and Russian.

Neighbourhoods from Daugavgrīva to Bišumuiža

We work in Āgenskalns, Klīversala and all the neighbourhoods of Pārdaugava: from Daugavgrīva and Bolderāja in the north to Bieriņi, Atgāzene and Bišumuiža in the south.
