Around Elvīras iela and between Dzirciema iela and the Bolderāja railway, estates of blocks of flats were built in the 1970s and 1980s. The streets between them still have wooden houses, the building of which began in the 1820s. In the first, the work usually starts at the riser and the small bathroom, in the second at the incoming supply pipe, old pipes and the yard drains.

An estate block or a wooden house: where the work starts

Building Where to look first What to know beforehand
A 1970s or 1980s multi-storey block the risers in the bathroom and kitchen, the joint with the flat's pipe, the heated towel rail where the flat's valves are and how to reach the building manager
A wooden house, built since the 1820s the incoming supply pipe into the house, pipes along outer walls, the pipe to the yard chamber where the house's main valve is and whether there is a basement
A low-rise building with several flats the shared pipe and under whose flat's floor it runs which neighbour to warn and who has the key to the basement

The residential areas of Zasulauks are in the centre of the neighbourhood, where multi-storey and low-rise buildings and some detached houses stand side by side. So one street can have a flat where you have to wait for the building manager's permission, and a house where the owner decides everything.

A riser in a 1970s block belongs to everyone, the pipe up to it to you

In a block of flats the cold and hot water risers and the soil stack run through all floors. Under the Apartment Ownership Law, the services belonging to the flat extend up to this riser, but the riser itself is common property. If it has not been changed since the building was built, a leak often appears right at the branch to the flat or where the riser passes through the floor slab.

A riser section can be replaced on one flat's floor too, but usually only with the consent of the flat owners' association, and the water has to be shut off for the whole riser for that time. In practice this is agreed with the building manager, who closes the valves in the basement and warns the neighbours. You, in turn, have to let into the flat the specialists authorised by the building manager if the work is needed for the building's services.

Who pays for a riser is determined by the building's documents and the association's decisions. Usually the association decides on common property, but ask your building manager; more in the guide who pays for riser replacement.

Apartment Ownership Law, Sections 3, 10 and 11. Checked 16.11.2026.

A bath or a shower in a small estate bathroom

In blocks of flats from those years the bathroom is usually narrow, and the bath takes up the whole wall. If a shower goes in instead of the bath, space is freed up, for example for a washing machine. In the plumbing part the most important thing is the height of the sewer outlet: a shower tray or drain needs a fall to the riser, and if the outlet is too high, the floor has to be raised or the outlet rebuilt.

Waterproofing and tiling are another trade and are not part of plumbing work. For a bath-to-shower conversion the price depends on removing the old bath, on whether it will be a cabin, a tray or a shower area with a drain, and on whether the sewer outlet has to be moved or lowered. It also depends on whether the water outlets and the mixer tap have to be moved, and on the floor level and access to the riser.

Radiators in the heating season, a towel rail all year

In a block of flats, radiators and a heated towel rail that depend on the building's shared system form part of the common property under the law. Replacing a radiator must be agreed with the building manager, and without a calculation you may not significantly increase the output or change the connection scheme.

Timing matters. In buildings run by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks, heating starts if for at least three days and nights the outdoor air is not warmer than +14 °C and has at some point cooled to +8 °C. Similar weather must also be expected for the next three days and nights. When the heating system is full, a radiator is not replaced until the building manager shuts off and drains the riser, so radiator work is better planned before the season. A heated towel rail on the hot water riser does not depend on heating: that riser is shut off in any season after approval by the building manager.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 4; the LV portāls e-consultation on radiator replacement; LSM on the start of the heating season in buildings run by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks. Checked 16.11.2026.

Peat under the centre and the pipe to the yard chamber

In the central part of Zasulauks there are peat deposits at a depth of 1–2 m, replaced with sand in built-up places, but in most of the neighbourhood groundwater is 1.5–3 m deep. For a wooden house with its own yard that means the pipe between the house and the chamber lies in ground whose composition can differ within one plot.

The yard pipe is usually the house owner's responsibility up to the outer wall of the inspection chamber; beyond it the Rīgas ūdens network begins. If water drains slowly from the sink and toilet across the whole house at once, the blockage is most likely in the yard, not in one trap. Then the pipe is cleared with a motorised drain snake from the house side or from the chamber, and if it recurs, a camera is used to find out whether tree roots have grown into the pipe or the pipe has lost its fall.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17, point 3. Checked 16.11.2026.

The neighbourhood is split by the Bolderāja railway line, and on both sides of it most of the territory is taken up by mixed development; the Zasulauks depot alone covers 13.9 ha. What people usually ask

The neighbour above replaced the riser, but the old one stayed on my floor. Is that allowed?

Technically a new section can be joined to the old one. The joint still has to be checked, because two materials meet there. Whether to replace the riser in the whole building at once or flat by flat is decided by the flat owners' association together with the building manager.

In a 1980s block, can a heated towel rail be replaced with an electric one?

It can, but a water-heated rail is often part of the hot water riser, so when it is removed, a bypass has to be fitted in its place so that circulation in the riser is not interrupted. That affects common property, so first talk to the building manager.

How do I tell whether my pipe is blocked or the whole wooden house's pipe to the chamber?

If only one sink drains slowly, the fault is usually in its trap or the short pipe. If the toilet gurgles and water rises in the shower at the same time, the blockage is most likely in the shared pipe or the yard. Clearing a blockage in the internal drains is from €50.

May a riser replacement be started in the middle of the heating season?

Heating does not directly affect the water and soil stack risers. The heating riser and radiators are a different matter: while the system is full, they are replaced only if the building manager shuts it off and drains it.
