A wooden detached house where the water pipe was added later

After the burning of 1812, when about 700 buildings in Pārdaugava burned down, about 1,000 low-rise wooden detached houses were built in Pārdaugava by 1819. Nobody then planned a bathroom or a water heater, so the water pipe and drains in such houses were added later, often in several layers and from different materials.

For the work that means one thing: before changing a tap, toilet or trap, the plumber has to understand where the pipe comes from and where the valve is that shuts off the whole house. If a stretch runs along an outer wall or in an unheated room, it can freeze in a hard frost; it makes sense to move such a place to a warmer wall or insulate it. A water heater needs a strong fixing, because a wooden partition often cannot hold a full water heater, and then it is hung on a load-bearing wall or on a separate frame.

Four things are useful in the request:

  • where the water pipe enters the house and whether the valve at the incoming supply pipe is reachable;
  • whether the house has a basement and what is in it (a water heater, a washing machine, a shower);
  • whether the pipes are visible or hidden under the floor and the cladding;
  • what was last changed in the house and roughly when.

A rental house with several flats and shared risers

In the workers' rental houses built at the end of the 19th century, the flats are joined by shared risers. The Apartment Ownership Law extends the flat as far as the common-property riser: the tap, trap, toilet and the pipe from them to the riser are yours, but the riser itself belongs to the whole building.

If the work affects a riser, the consent of the flat owners' association is needed, and in practice you first talk about it with the building manager. One old building can contain a completely rebuilt flat as well as a flat with pipes that have not been changed for a long time. Then the place where a new plastic pipe is joined to the old riser is the first to look at if something starts to seep.

If water is already leaking at the neighbour below, shut off your flat's valve, unplug the appliances nearby and warn the neighbour. What to do next is described on the page about a flooded flat.

Apartment Ownership Law, Sections 3 and 11: the flat's services extend up to the common-property risers; work that affects common property needs the consent of the flat owners' association. Checked 16.11.2026.

Basements on made ground on Mūkusala and Bieķensala

In the northern and eastern parts of Torņakalns, including Mūkusala and Bieķensala, sand alternates with 3–10 m thick silt, and the built-up areas are covered with 4–6 m of fill. Around Māras dīķis groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m, while in the south-west, on the Torņakalns dunes, it is mostly 1.5–3 m deep and deeper.

For plumbing, this fact becomes important in the basement. If you want a shower, sink, washing machine or floor drain in a basement or semi-basement, first find out whether that appliance will be lower than the lid of the nearest sewer inspection chamber in the yard. If it will, Riga's regulations require a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve so that wastewater cannot flow back into the basement.

The other rule concerns water that collects in the basement: groundwater and rainwater may not be discharged into the drains without approval. So a drainage pump is not connected to the household drains just because they are closer.

Source: Riga binding regulations No. 17, points 63.8 and 63.9, likumi.lv, checked 16.11.2026.

Water on the floor usually comes from inside the house

According to apkaimes.lv data, flood risk in Torņakalns practically does not exist; the exception is some places by the Kīleveins ditch, where it is up to 2%. So a puddle in the kitchen or bathroom here most likely comes from the house's own system, and it can be found by checking the supply pipes methodically.

  1. Under the sink the nuts of the flexible hose at the mixer tap and the trap joints; a damp edge on the bottom shelf is the first sign.
  2. At the washing machine the inlet hose at the tap and the outlet hose where it is inserted in the drain outlet.
  3. At the water heater the safety valve usually drips when the water heats up; if it leaks constantly, the valve or the pressure has to be checked.
  4. At the toilet the supply tap and the cistern's fill valve; water that runs constantly into the toilet is also a leak.

A neighbourhood between the railway lines and Vienības gatve

Torņakalns is divided by the Riga-Jelgava and Riga-Tukums railway lines and Vienības gatve, so an exact address is useful in the form, not only the name of the neighbourhood. Torņakalns borders Āgenskalns, Atgāzene, Bieriņi and Ziepniekkalns, and the Old Town can be reached over the Stone Bridge. All the neighbourhoods where we work are on the page plumber in Pārdaugava.

What people usually ask

In an old wooden house, can I replace just one leaking section of pipe?

Yes, if the rest of the pipe is in good condition. Fixing a leak by replacing a joint, seal or short section in an accessible place is from €40. If the inspection shows that similar joints are starting to seep elsewhere too, the plumber says so, and you decide whether to replace a longer section.

Who owns the pipe between the street and my house in Torņakalns?

Riga's regulations No. 17 extend the Rīgas ūdens network up to the outer wall of the commercial metering chamber or the plot boundary, and for sewerage up to the outer wall of the inspection chamber. Beyond that the networks are usually the owner's responsibility. If it is not clear where this boundary lies for your house, ask Rīgas ūdens.

In a rental house flat, may I move the bathroom appliances elsewhere?

In a flat, as far as the work does not affect common property, appliances can be moved without the other owners' consent; whether construction documents are needed, ask the building manager or the building board. If the connection to the riser or the riser itself has to be changed, the consent of the flat owners' association is needed; ask the building manager how that works in your building.
