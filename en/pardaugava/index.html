A plumber in Pārdaugava, Riga's left bank, works in very different buildings: in Daugavgrīva and Bolderāja they are Soviet-era multi-storey blocks, in Zasulauks and Ķīpsala old wooden houses, in Bieriņi and Beberbeķi detached houses. Left-bank buildings and their pipes

In the north, Soviet-era multi-storey blocks dominate: in Bolderāja panel buildings were built from 1965, in Daugavgrīva most of the wooden buildings were demolished after the war and blocks of flats were built, and in Dzirciems the largest housing estate appeared in the 1980s. In these buildings the work often starts at the riser and the building manager.

In the middle is the old Pārdaugava. In Āgenskalns and Torņakalns most of the buildings appeared after the fire of 1812, and in Zasulauks many wooden houses of that time still stand. The northern part of Ķīpsala and the historic part of Āgenskalns are urban planning monuments of national importance.

In the south and west are the neighbourhoods of private houses. The Bieriņi neighbourhood includes a city district called Mārupe (within Riga, not Mārupe municipality). There and in Bolderāja, Rīgas ūdens built new networks in 2012–2015, and in Bišumuiža too, since 2018, connections to these phase 4 networks have been supported. For a house connection to the drains, the municipality offers co-financing of up to €2,000, depending on the length of the supply line. Kleisti is the biggest neighbourhood in Riga, but only 462 people live in it; in Spilve and Voleri there are very few homes.

Klīversala is not a separate neighbourhood: it is part of the Āgenskalns neighbourhood by the Akmens bridge. There, at the northern tip of Ķīpsala and in most of Bolderāja, the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. For a basement or a ground-floor level this means one Riga rule: a sink, toilet or drain that is lower than the cover of the nearest sewer inspection chamber may only be connected with a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. RD-23-207-sn (co-financing by supply line length) and No. 17, point 63.8 (non-return valve); data from apkaimes.lv. Checked on 16.11.2026.
