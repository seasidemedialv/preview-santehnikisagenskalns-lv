Plumber in Ķīpsala, Riga - historic wooden houses and flats in a former factory

A plumber in Ķīpsala, Riga, meets two very different kinds of building: two-storey wooden rental houses and detached houses that replaced the fishermen's log cabins in an eclectic or Art Nouveau spirit, and newer flats in converted buildings.

In a wooden house the job revolves around the timber floor between neighbours and pipes that were added later. In a newer flat it is a concealed toilet, hidden pipes and joints that have to be pressure-tested.

A two-storey wooden rental house: the neighbour lives right under the floor

In a wooden rental house, only a timber floor separates the second-floor bathroom from the ceiling of the first floor. A leak that stays in the floor for a while in a concrete building shows up here quickly in the neighbour's ceiling and wall finish. So the first step is the same as in any building, only more urgent: shut off the valve, wipe up the water and warn the neighbour below.

  1. Find the valve The flat's valve is usually by the incoming supply pipe in the bathroom or kitchen; if the flat has no valve of its own, the water has to be shut off for the whole house at the incoming supply pipe.
  2. Switch off the appliances Switch off the water heater and the washing machine, and do not pour water down the sink until it is clear whether the drain is leaking.
  3. Show the place Photograph the wet spot on your floor and on the neighbour's ceiling; it will help both the plumber and the conversation with the neighbour.

If the water has already reached the floor below, the page on a flooded flat helps. In a house with several flats the shared pipes are common property, so they have to be discussed with the building manager or the other owners.

An urban planning monument: what plumbing touches and what it does not

The low-rise housing in the north of the island, dating from the turn of the 19th and 20th centuries, is an urban planning monument of national importance, and the whole of Ķīpsala lies in the protection zone of the UNESCO site “Historic Centre of Riga”. Replacing a tap, toilet or water heater happens inside the house and usually does not affect the outside of the building.

It is different with work that reaches outside: a new pipe through the facade, a ventilation outlet, a drain line towards the street or a new incoming supply pipe in the yard. Before such work, find out which approvals your building needs; the plumber can say what is technically possible, but the question of permission is the building owner's.

Inside the house, in a historic interior, people usually try not to leave pipes visible: they run along the skirting boards, behind the panelling or under the floor. That makes the work more expensive, because the finish has to be opened and restored, and this part is not included in the plumbing price.

The former gypsum factory and other newer flats

In 2003 a complex of flats was created in the former gypsum factory, the first example in Latvia of a production building converted into flats. In these and other newer flats there are no old-riser problems, but there are others: a concealed toilet with its frame behind the wall, hidden mixer taps, long plastic pipes in the floor.

  • The cistern leaks into the toilet: in a concealed cistern the mechanism is replaced through the button opening, without opening the wall.
  • Pressure drops or the meter turns when the taps are closed: the pipe in the floor has to be pressure-tested before the leak is looked for.
  • A new toilet at a finished wall: the frame can be installed and connected, but plasterboard cladding and tiles are separate.

Soft ground and groundwater close to the surface at the north end of the island

The ground of Ķīpsala has low bearing capacity to a depth of more than 15 m. At the north end of the island groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m, elsewhere mostly at 1.5–3 m; the north end and the strip along the Zunds are raised by washed-up sand, on average to 2–3 m.

If the house has a basement or semi-basement where you plan a shower, washing machine or floor drain, first compare its floor with the lid of the nearest sewer inspection chamber. For appliances that are lower, Riga's regulations require a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve. Groundwater from a basement may not be discharged into the drains without approval.

Binding regulations No. 17, points 63.8 and 63.9, apply to basement appliances and groundwater; text on likumi.lv checked 16.11.2026.

Bridges to Dzirciems, Āgenskalns and across the Daugava

Ķīpsala is an island between the Daugava and the Zunds, and bridges connect it with Dzirciems, Āgenskalns, the Centre and the Old Town; the Vanšu Bridge opened in 1981. The southern part has the Press House, Riga Technical University faculties and the “Ķīpsala” exhibition centre, while the detached-house areas are on the island's historic eastern side. In the Āgenskalns neighbourhood, by the Stone Bridge, there is also Klīversala, and you will find all the neighbourhoods on the page plumber in Pārdaugava.

What people usually ask

In a monument area, may I replace a bath or water heater without a permit?

Replacing appliances inside a flat usually does not affect the outside of the building. If the work needs a new hole in the facade, a new incoming supply pipe or changes in the yard, first find out which approvals your own building needs.

In a wooden house the water was leaking at my place, but the stain is in the neighbour's ceiling in a different spot. How come?

In a timber floor, water tends to run away along a beam or the joints between boards, so the stain is not always directly under the leak. The plumber searches from above: supply pipes, traps, bath and shower joints and the edge of the floor.

My concealed toilet leaks, but I do not want to tear out the tiles. What can be done?

Most of the cistern mechanism can be replaced through the button opening. Cistern repair is from €35, for a concealed cistern it can cost more. If the frame's supply pipe behind the wall is leaking, the inspection tells you whether it can be done without opening.
