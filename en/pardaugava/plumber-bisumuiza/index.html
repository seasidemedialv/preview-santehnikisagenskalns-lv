Bišumuiža is one of the neighbourhoods where connections to the phase 4 Cohesion Fund water services networks have been supported since 2018. So many jobs here are about how to join a house's drains and water supply to the city network and what happens with the yard drain and the cellar.

Connecting to the phase 4 network step by step

The connection process as a whole includes technical conditions, a design, approvals, construction and handover, and it usually takes about 4–6 months, in some cases longer. The plumber is only one part of it; the sequence is usually as follows.

  1. Check the address on vaitevtovajag.lv you can find out whether the service pipe has been built up to your plot.
  2. Technical conditions and design the process starts with them; work in the city system may only be done under the supervision of a water supply and sewerage construction supervisor who has the rights needed for this work.
  3. Service pipe on the plot the pipe from the house to the inspection chamber or chamber at the plot boundary is the owner's part, built according to the design.
  4. Work inside the house the internal drains and water supply have to be prepared for the new connection: the outlet from the house, the falls, the cleanout points.
  5. Contract and handover after construction a contract is signed with Rīgas ūdens; co-financing is paid out after construction.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17, clauses 3 and 75; LV portāls on co-financing conditions and the duration of the connection. Checked 16.11.2026.

Co-financing by service pipe length

Riga City Council regulations RD-23-207-sn provide co-financing for connecting a residential house to the centralised sewerage by service pipe length. It is paid out after construction, if at least one person is declared as resident at the property and a contract has been signed with Rīgas ūdens. No co-financing is provided for a water supply connection.

Sewer service pipe length Co-financing
up to 5 m €1,000
5–20 m €1,500
over 20 m €2,000

If the connection requires work within the street's red lines, Rīgas ūdens offers additional support of up to €12,000 for building the sewer service pipe. Ask Rīgas ūdens for the exact conditions for your address, as they can change.

RD-23-207-sn (likumi.lv); LV portāls releases on the phase 4 neighbourhoods and the Rīgas ūdens campaign. Checked 16.11.2026.

A cellar in the Daugava valley and by the Bišumuiža ditch

In the northern part of the neighbourhood, in the Daugava valley, there is loose sand with silt layers from 1–3 m to 6–8 m thick, and the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. In built-up places the ground has been replaced with several metres of fill. Around the Bišumuiža ditch, which crosses the neighbourhood, the groundwater level is also high. In the Ziepniekkalns dune part, though, the groundwater is deeper than 1.5 m and the building conditions are fairly favourable.

If a house has a cellar with a sink, shower, washing machine or drain, before connecting to the city network it has to be measured whether these fixtures are lower than the cover of the nearest sewer inspection chamber. If they are, Riga's regulations require a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve. Drainage or rainwater may not be discharged into the domestic drains without approval, so a cellar pump is not connected to the new pipe just because it is convenient.

Valve requirement and groundwater ban: regulations No. 17, clauses 63.8 and 63.9, viewed on likumi.lv on 16.11.2026.

Your own well and the city water supply in one house

If the house has so far had its own borehole or well and the city water supply is now being connected, the two systems have to be separated. Riga's regulations forbid connecting a borehole system to the city water supply. If you keep the borehole, for example for the garden, it must be a completely separate pipe with its own taps.

In the house, the old pressure booster pump or pump line has to be disconnected from the internal pipe and checked so that no link between the two systems has been left anywhere.

Separating the borehole and the city water supply is required by regulations No. 17, clause 63.6 (likumi.lv, checked 16.11.2026).

What people usually ask

Can the plumber build the whole connection up to the street?

In Riga, work in the city system is led by a water supply and sewerage construction supervisor with the right to lead such work, and it is done according to a design. The plumber prepares the house's internal drains and water supply, switches the fixtures over and clears the yard drain; building the external service pipe is ordered from whoever works to an approved design.

What should be done with the old local system after connecting to the city drains?

The house outlet is switched to the new service pipe so that the wastewater no longer goes to the old tank. Emptying and decommissioning the tank is a separate service; the plumber's work is the house's internal pipes and the yard drain.

Is co-financing due if nobody is declared as resident in the house?

Under RD-23-207-sn, co-financing is paid out if at least one person is declared as resident at the property and a contract has been signed with Rīgas ūdens. If these conditions are not met, ask Rīgas ūdens or the municipality about your case.

In a Bišumuiža house the water drains slowly in all the sinks at once. Where should I look?

If several fixtures drain slowly at the same time, the blockage is usually in the shared outlet from the house or in the yard drain, not in a single trap. Yard drain clearing is from €50 for the first hour; if it recurs, the pipe can be inspected with a camera.
