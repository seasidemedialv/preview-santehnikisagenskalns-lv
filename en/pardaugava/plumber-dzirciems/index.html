There the jobs revolve around risers that are about forty years old, radiators on central heating and small bathrooms. The other, smaller part is the historic wooden and brick houses at the start of Buļļu iela near Dzegužkalns.

The estate between the railway and Dzirciema iela

In the 1980s Dzirciema iela, the extension of Jūrmalas gatve and the blocks of flats were built here. These buildings have shared risers for cold water, hot water and drains, and the radiators are usually connected to the building's central heating. If the risers have not been changed since construction, they are about forty years old, and it pays to replace them together with a bathroom renovation.

The best moment to replace a riser section is before tiling, while the pipe is still visible. Afterwards access costs more: a box or tiles have to be opened. Because the riser is the building's common property, the work is agreed with the building manager, who shuts off the water in the basement.

A radiator on central heating: not only your business

In Dzirciems blocks of flats, a radiator is usually connected to the building's heating riser. The law counts such a heating element as common property if its operation depends on the building's shared services. That is why replacing a radiator in a block of flats must be agreed with the building manager, and without a calculation you may not significantly increase the radiator's output or change the connection scheme.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 4; the LV portāls e-consultation on approval of radiator replacement. Checked 16.11.2026.

The other matter is timing. In buildings run by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks, heating starts when the air has not warmed above +14 °C for at least three days and nights in a row and has at some point cooled to +8 °C. The same weather must also be expected for the next three. Once the system is full, replacing a radiator means shutting off and draining the riser, which only the building manager can do. If at all possible, plan the replacement for a time when the heating is not yet on. More on approval in the guide replacing radiators in a flat and approval.

The small bathroom: bath out, shower in

In a block of flats the bathroom is usually narrow, and the bath takes up a whole wall. When you replace it with a shower cabin or tray, space appears for a washing machine. Two things have to be settled: whether the sewer outlet is low enough for the shower drain to have a fall, and whether the heated towel rail hung on the hot water riser stays in place.

  • If the shower tray is low and the outlet is high, the tray has to be raised on legs or the outlet lowered.
  • If the riser is behind the bath, now is the time to make sure it does not leak.
  • Waterproofing and tiling are another trade and are not included in plumbing work.

If the washing machine moves from the kitchen to the bathroom, it needs a water tap and a drain outlet in the new place. That is one extra point on both the water pipe and the drains. Think about it at the same time as the shower, not after tiling, because later the pipe will have to be run on the surface or chased into the wall.

The start of Buļļu iela: old wooden and brick houses

The historic low-rise wooden and brick housing in Dzirciems is best preserved at the start of Buļļu iela near Dzegužkalns and on some stretches of Dzirciema iela. In these houses the plumbing was usually installed later than the building was built, so pipes tend to run on the surface or through places that get cold in winter. Before hanging a water heater on a wooden partition, check that the wall will carry it. More: plumbing in an old wooden house.

The former Riga Hospital No. 6 on Dzirciema iela, which operated until 1997, has been converted into a municipal block of flats. So Dzirciems also has this type of building: a large public building in which flats were later fitted.

Why groundwater is rarely an issue in Dzirciems

Unlike Bolderāja and Daugavgrīva, in the central part of Dzirciems building conditions are favourable, and in places groundwater is deeper than 3 m. Ground with low bearing capacity is found only in the Daugava valley between Daugavgrīvas iela and Zunds, where the development is mainly industrial. So in flats here a leak is usually sought in pipes, joints and risers, not in the basement from outside.

What people usually ask

Can a radiator in Dzirciems be replaced in the middle of the heating season?

It can, if the building manager shuts off and drains the heating riser. That also affects the neighbours in the same vertical line, so it is done rarely. It is simpler to plan the work before heating starts.

May the new radiator be larger than the old one?

Without a calculation you may not significantly increase the output or change the connection scheme, and the replacement is agreed with the building manager. If a room is cold, first check whether the old radiator heats across its full area at all.

Can I replace the riser only in my own flat if the others do not?

A one-floor section can be replaced, but only with the building manager's approval, because the riser is common property. The joints with the neighbours' old pipes above and below have to be made carefully, because that is where leaks tend to appear later.
