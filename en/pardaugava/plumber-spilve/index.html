Most of the land is taken up by freeport territory, the airfield, meadows and family allotment gardens. If you have a house or a garden cabin in Spilve or Podrags, what matters most is the wet ground, protecting the water pipe from frost and where the wastewater goes.

The freeport, the airfield and family allotments

By type of use, the largest share of Spilve's area, 34.1%, is territory belonging to the Freeport of Riga, including most of the Spilve airfield, the meadows and the family allotment gardens. The airfield, set up in the 1920s, is now used by private aircraft. Podrags is also counted as part of the neighbourhood.

That is why this page is short. With 69 residents in the whole neighbourhood, plumbing questions here are most often about an individual house or garden cabin: the water supply pipe, a tap, a water heater, the outlet from the kitchen and toilet to the yard.

Land almost at sea level: how it affects pipes

The relief of Spilve is distinctly flat, mostly 0–2 m above sea level, and most of it is a marshy plain between two dune ridges. Almost everywhere the ground has weak bearing capacity: sand alternates with 3–10 m thick silt, and groundwater is very high. The territory is drained by the Hapaka grāvis, the Spilves grāvis and the Lāčupīte together with the polder's drainage ditches, and in a wide area the flood risk reaches 10% if the hydraulic structures are not maintained.

For plumbing this means that a pipe in the ground can settle, and then the pipe loses its fall and starts to block in one and the same place. If the house is connected to the network and an outlet is lower than the lid of the nearest inspection chamber, a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve is needed. Groundwater from drainage may not be discharged into the drains without approval.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17, points 63.8 and 63.9. Checked 16.11.2026.

The service pipe from the street to the house is yours

Under Riga's regulations, the Rīgas ūdens water network ends at the outer wall of the commercial metering chamber or at the plot boundary, and for sewerage at the outer wall of the inspection chamber. Everything beyond that up to the house and in the house is the owner's responsibility. On soft, wet ground, exactly this stretch and the place where the pipe enters the house through the foundation are the first to look at if the pressure drops or a wet patch appears in the yard without rain.

Binding regulations No. 17 (15.12.2017), point 3. Checked 16.11.2026.

A garden cabin before winter

In an unheated cabin in winter the water in a pipe freezes and can burst it. Before the cold, shut off the supply pipe, open the taps and let the water out of the pipes, and out of the water heater too if it stays in an unheated room. In spring, when you turn the water back on, watch the joints for the first hour: after winter a small leak can appear in the joints, and it is better to fix it at once, not a week later.

Where the wastewater goes if there is no city sewer

Our reading of the map (OpenStreetMap): the Rīgas ūdens sewage pumping station at Daugavgrīvas iela 101 is in Podrags, Spilve. According to Rīgas ūdens, this station pumps wastewater on to the Daugavgrīva treatment plant, where a reception point for decentralised wastewater has also opened, to which the contents of holding tanks are taken.

Whether your exact address is on the network, check on vaitevtovajag.lv or with Rīgas ūdens. If the house has a holding tank, emptying it is a different service. The plumber's part is the indoor pipes up to the tank and clearing the yard line if it is blocked.

What people usually ask

I only have a garden cabin in Spilve. Can I book a plumber?

Yes. In the form write what is in the cabin (a tap, a water heater, a toilet) and what does not work. After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

A pipe froze and burst in winter. What should I do before the plumber comes?

Shut off the supply pipe and, if there is a water heater, switch off its electricity. Do not heat the pipe with an open flame. Then describe in the form where the break is and whether the pipe is in the ground or indoors.

May a drainage pump be connected to the drains?

Riga's regulations forbid discharging groundwater and rainwater into the drains without approval. So drainage water needs another route or an approval.

The house has a holding tank. Does the plumber empty it?

No, emptying a tank is a sewage-collection job. The plumber works with pipes: the house's indoor drains and the yard line up to the tank, if it is blocked or has lost its fall.
