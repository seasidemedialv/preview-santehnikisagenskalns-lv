Plumber in Atgāzene, Riga - homes by the stop and peaty ground

A plumber in Atgāzene works in Riga's smallest neighbourhood: 74.5 ha between Vienības gatve, the railway and Kārļa Ulmaņa gatve, where 1,449 people lived in 2025.

The homes are mostly in the north-west by the Atgāzene railway stop, and there is also a small area of private houses there. Along Vienības gatve residential buildings alternate with shopping centres and offices, so plumbing work depends on whether your building has one flat or several.

On Ābolu iela one side is Riga, the other Mārupe municipality

One of the neighbourhood's boundaries follows the city boundary, Ābolu iela and Dzelzceļa iela. On Ābolu iela the odd-numbered side has Riga numbering, while the even-numbered side has Mārupe parish addresses, and the buildings there merge with those of Mārupe municipality. You cannot see this from the street, but it matters for planning plumbing.

For Riga addresses, Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17 on water services and Riga's co-financing for a sewer connection apply. Homes on the other side of the street are in Mārupe municipality, so Riga's regulations and co-financing do not apply to them; ask the Mārupe municipality about how the service pipe and connection are handled there. That is why you should enter the house number in the form, not just the street name.

Peat by the source of Bieriņgrāvis and pipes in the ground

Bieriņgrāvis, also called Melnupīte, rises in the centre of Atgāzene and is the neighbourhood's main drainage. There peat, also silty, lies up to 3 m deep in the ground, and the groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. In built-up places the peat has been replaced with sand, but the terrain across the whole neighbourhood is distinctly flat, mostly 10–11 m above sea level.

On flat ground the land does not give the yard drain its fall; it has to be created by how the pipe is laid. If the slope has been lost in some section, deposits collect there and the pipe blocks again and again. Sand fill that has replaced peat freezes deeper than clay, and that has to be taken into account if the pipe is laid anew.

Sewerage structures building code and LBN 003-19: for a yard gravity drain (up to 500 mm), the depth is calculated from the frost depth that can occur in the ground once in a hundred years, minus 0.3 m. However, there must be at least 0.7 m of soil above the pipe, and if there is not, the pipe is insulated and protected from loads. In Riga the once-in-100-years frost depth of clayey ground is 120 cm, and for sandy ground a factor of 1.2 is used. Checked 16.11.2026.

If the building has several flats: yours up to the riser, shared beyond it

Section 3 of the Apartment Ownership Law states that the flat includes the utility lines up to the common-property risers and the bath, shower and toilet equipment. The riser itself and the heating elements whose operation depends on the building's systems are common property under Section 4. So you can replace a mixer tap, a toilet or an outlet up to the riser yourself, but replacing a section of the riser needs the consent of the flat owners' association, which is usually arranged through the building manager.

If water appears in the neighbour's ceiling below you, first turn off the valve in your flat and check whether it is leaking in your flat. If the valve does not stop the flow or the leak is from the riser, contact the building manager: the riser in the basement is shut off only by them or a person they authorise. After that, describe in the form where the water appears and which floor you live on.

A private house by the stop: check the connection by address

Whether a particular street in Atgāzene is on the city sewer network, we cannot say from a distance. The address can be checked on vaitevtovajag.lv or by asking Rīgas ūdens. In Riga more than 8,000 residents are still not connected to centralised water supply and sewerage, so the question is not an empty one.

  • If the service pipe up to the plot boundary has been built, for a new building that uses water, connecting to the city system is mandatory (regulations No. 17, clause 5).
  • The whole property is connected, not individual flats; for a house with several flats, the technical conditions are requested by the building manager.
  • If work has to be done in the street within the red lines, Rīgas ūdens offers additional support of up to €12,000 for building the sewer service pipe.
  • If the house has a septic tank, the yard pipe between the house and the tank can be cleared with a motorised drain snake when it is blocked.

What people usually ask

The house is on Ābolu iela. Which municipality's rules apply?

It depends on the number. Odd numbers are in Riga, and Riga's water services regulations and co-financing apply to them. Even numbers are Mārupe parish addresses; ask Mārupe municipality about the service pipe and connection there. If in doubt, check the address in the property documents.

A stain appeared on the neighbour's ceiling below me. What do I do first?

Turn off the valve in your flat, look under the bath, sink and behind the toilet and dry the visible water. If nothing is leaking in your flat, the fault may be in the riser or higher up, and then the building manager has to be informed. After that, fill in the form with a description.

Can a shower or toilet be installed in a cellar on peaty ground?

It can, but fixtures lower than the cover of the nearest inspection chamber need a separate outlet and a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve, otherwise the street sewer can flood the cellar. Where the groundwater is shallow, the place where the outlet passes through the cellar wall and its sealing also matter.
