Plumber in Voleri, Riga - a row of houses along the Daugava dyke

A plumber call-out in Voleri, Riga, usually means a low-rise house: the residential development here is a row of single and two-storey houses along a dyke built on the Daugava bank, and it took shape in the 1930s.

In 2025 the neighbourhood had 211 residents, and almost half of the land is taken up by the port and industry. For houses by the river the main questions are shallow groundwater, frost on old service pipes and where the wastewater goes.

Houses in the middle of the port territory

Industrial and port territories take up 47.3% of Voleri. For the residential zone along the Daugava in the port territory, mixed development is permitted, which allows the residential development to be kept. Voleri also includes Krēmeri, Lejas Podrags and the uninhabited Krievu sala, and by land the neighbourhood borders only Bolderāja and Spilve.

If a house was built in the 1930s, the water pipe and drains were usually added later and reworked several times. So one house can have a new plastic pipe as well as an old steel or cast-iron section. Before bigger work it is worth finding out where the main shut-off valve is and where the service pipe runs from the street.

Groundwater is closer than one and a half metres

The land in Voleri mostly rises only 1–3 m above the sea, and some places flood regularly. Sand alternates with 3–10 m thick silt, in places to a depth of 20–30 m, and groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. In most of the neighbourhood the flood risk is high, in some cases up to 10%.

That changes the plumber's work in two places. First, in a basement or a low ground floor, a sewer outlet needs a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve if it is lower than the lid of the nearest inspection chamber. Second, a pipe laid in the ground can settle in soft ground, and then a blockage recurs in one and the same place. Groundwater from drainage may not be discharged into the drains without approval.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17, points 63.8 and 63.9. Checked 16.11.2026.

When water has been in the house

  1. Electricity first If it can be done safely, without standing in water, switch off the electricity at the fuse board; do not touch flooded appliances. Do not switch on the water heater and pumps until an electrician has checked them.
  2. Drains and floor drains After the water has receded, check that the sink, shower and toilet drain normally. If the water stands or comes back, the pipe may be silted with sand.
  3. Service pipe and joints Look at the visible joints in the basement and at the place where the pipe enters the house. If something seeps, shut off the main valve and book the repair.

Whether the house is on the city sewer

Whether your house in Voleri is on the Rīgas ūdens sewer network can be checked by address on vaitevtovajag.lv. If the network reaches the plot, the municipality pays co-financing for connecting the house to the central sewer depending on the length of the service pipe: up to 5 m €1,000, 5–20 m €1,500, over 20 m €2,000. It is paid out after construction, if at least one person is registered at the property and a contract has been signed with Rīgas ūdens.

If the connection requires work within the street's red lines, Rīgas ūdens offers additional support of up to €12,000 for building the sewer service pipe. In this process the plumber works in the house and the yard: gathers the indoor outlets in one place and re-routes them to the new service pipe.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. RD-23-207-sn on co-financing. Checked 16.11.2026.

What people usually ask

In a Voleri house a pipe freezes by the outer wall in winter. What can be done?

The pipe can be moved to a warmer inner wall, or the section along the outer wall can be insulated. Which option suits depends on the wall and the route of the pipe; you talk it through with the plumber on the call and at the inspection.

After high water the toilet drains slowly. Is it a blockage?

Often yes: sand and mud can have settled in the pipe between the house and the chamber. If all the appliances drain slowly at the same time, the fault is usually in the yard line, not in the toilet.

Is there a city sewer in Voleri for my house?

It is best to check by address on vaitevtovajag.lv or with Rīgas ūdens. If the network is available, you can receive municipal co-financing for the connection if the conditions of the regulations are met.
