Plumber in Bolderāja, Riga - panel blocks, the old village and private houses

A plumber in Bolderāja, Riga, most often works in three kinds of building: the panel-block district that started going up in 1965, the 1950s sand-lime brick factory village around Silikātu iela, and private houses in a neighbourhood where Rīgas ūdens built new networks in 2012–2015.

Each has its own weak point: the shared riser, an old flat pipe or the yard sewer. Below is what the plumber does in each case and what you need to sort out yourself.

The panel district: the riser belongs to the whole building

Building of the panel blocks in Bolderāja began in 1965, under a plan for two districts meant to house 15 thousand people. In such a building the water and sewer risers run through all floors in one vertical line and are part of the building's common property. The flat owns only the pipes from the riser to your taps, toilet and bath.

In practice that means two things. First, if a joint leaks after the first valve in the flat, it can be repaired straight away and you do not need the building manager. Second, if the riser itself or its tee leaks, the water is shut off in the basement, and that is done by the building manager or a person the manager authorises. Replacing the section of the riser on one floor is possible, but only after approval, because it affects the whole building's property.

Apartment Ownership Law, Sections 3 and 11: the flat's part is the service lines up to the common-property risers; work that affects the risers needs the consent of the flat owners' association. Checked 16.11.2026.

If one stairwell has flats where the risers have already been replaced and flats with the old pipes, the joint between the new and the old section is a place the plumber inspects with extra care. Ask the neighbours above and below what is behind their tiles or boxing before you start tiling a new bathroom.

The Silikātu iela village and old wooden buildings

Before the panel blocks, Bolderāja already had a village for sand-lime brick factory workers, built in the early 1950s with standard flats around Silikātu iela, and earlier still old wooden housing: in 1909 there were 280 wooden and 20 brick buildings here. Part of this oldest housing has survived and is regarded as a cultural and historical asset.

In low-rise brick and wooden houses the plumbing may have been rebuilt several times, and pipes from different years can meet in one wall. In a wooden house you also need to check whether the water pipe runs along an outer wall, where it can freeze in winter, and whether the partition will carry a heavy water heater. We write about this in more detail in the guide plumbing in an old wooden house.

Groundwater close to the surface: if there is a sink or shower in the basement

In Bolderāja groundwater is mostly at a depth of 0–1.5 m, and deeper than 3 m only in the dune belt. In the northern part of the neighbourhood, between the railway and Lielā iela, lies the Buļļupe floodplain meadow, which floods regularly. For houses closer to these places, the basement and the lowest plumbing points are the first thing to check.

Riga's binding regulations forbid connecting a sink, toilet, floor drain or drainage that sits lower than the lid of the nearest sewer inspection chamber to the drains without a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve. If the water level in the network rises, without such a valve it can come out through your basement floor drain. The same regulations forbid discharging groundwater into the drains without approval, so a basement drainage pump must not simply be connected to the sewer pipe.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17 (15.12.2017), points 63.8 and 63.9. Checked 16.11.2026.

Private houses near the new networks: where Rīgas ūdens' part ends

In phase 4 of the Cohesion Fund project, Rīgas ūdens built new networks in Bolderāja too in 2012–2015. For a private house owner who connects to the central sewer, the municipality offers co-financing by the length of the service pipe: up to 5 m €1,000, 5–20 m €1,500, over 20 m €2,000. The whole connection process, with technical conditions, a design and handover, usually takes about 4–6 months.

Rīgas ūdens' responsibility ends at the outer wall of the inspection chamber or at the plot boundary. Everything from there to the house belongs to the owner. That is usually where a plumber has work: the yard sewer line blocks, the indoor drains have to be re-routed in place of the old outlet, or you need to find where a pipe loses its fall. The service pipe to the central system itself is built to a design and under the supervision of a water supply and sewerage works manager, which is a separate job.

Binding regulations No. 17, points 3 and 75; Riga City Council binding regulations No. RD-23-207-sn on co-financing. Checked 16.11.2026.

What people usually ask

In a panel block in Bolderāja, may I replace the riser only on my own floor?

Technically a one-floor section can be replaced, but the riser is common property, so the work is agreed with the building manager and the water is shut off in the basement by the manager's person. Who pays is set by the building's documents; usually ask the building manager. Read more in the guide who pays for riser replacement.

After rain, water comes up from the floor drain in my basement. What should I do?

First find out whether the drain is lower than the nearest sewer inspection chamber lid. If it is, Riga's regulations require a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve. In the form, write how deep the basement is and what is connected in it, and on the call you talk through the job and the price.

I am connecting to the new sewer. What does the plumber do and what does the design cover?

The service pipe up to the Rīgas ūdens inspection chamber is built to the technical conditions and the design. The plumber works inside the house and on sections of the yard line: re-routes outlets, clears blockages and repairs joints.
