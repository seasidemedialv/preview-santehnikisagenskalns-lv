Post-war blocks of flats in an old street grid

The street network of Daugavgrīva comes from a plan of 1846: four streets parallel to the Buļļupe and four cross streets, which has survived to this day. After the Second World War the neighbourhood became a closed military zone, the wooden houses were mostly demolished, and blocks of flats were built in their place. It is precisely the blocks of the 1950s and 1960s that dominate the neighbourhood these days.

In a building this old, one stairwell can hold several generations of plumbing. One flat has long since switched its pipes to plastic, another may still use the original steel water pipe and cast-iron drain. Before you change a toilet or a bath, it is worth looking at the condition of the branch to the riser, because a new toilet on an old, scaled-up pipe does not solve the problem.

The line between you and the building is usually clear: the flat's service lines up to the common-property risers are yours, while the risers and the building's systems are common property, whose maintenance and repair are the building manager's responsibility.

If the water has already reached the neighbour below

  1. Shut off the flat's valves The cold and hot water valves are usually by the meters in the toilet or bathroom. If the leak then stops, the fault is in your branch.
  2. If it still leaks, the fault is higher up or in the riser Then the water is coming from the neighbour above or from the riser itself. The riser is shut off in the basement by the building manager or a person the manager authorises.
  3. Warn the neighbour below you Ask them to lift their belongings, switch off the electricity in the damaged room and photograph the ceiling. The photos will help with the flooding report.
  4. Let them in If the damage is in common property, the law requires you to let the building manager's authorised specialists into the flat so that they can repair the building's services.

What to do in the first hour and how to draw up the flooding report is described in detail in the guide water leaking from the flat above. For repairing the damage itself, read the page on a flooded flat.

Wastewater from the whole of Riga ends up on Buļļu Island

Between the Buļļupe and the Ziemeļupe, on Dzintara iela, stands the Daugavgrīva biological treatment plant, which treats wastewater from the whole of Riga and part of the Riga region. It was commissioned in 1991 and covers 52 ha. It changes nothing directly for your flat, but it is a reminder that the drains are not a rubbish bin.

Riga's regulations forbid discharging solid objects, building materials, textiles, sand and fats into the drains. In an old flat with a cast-iron branch, kitchen sink grease sticks to the rough walls of the pipe, and over time the water leaves the sink more and more slowly. If the blockage is in your branch, it is cleared with a drain snake. If the water also rises at your neighbours', the fault is in the riser, and then you call the building manager. How to tell one from the other is in the guide why the water does not drain.

Binding regulations No. 17 (15.12.2017), point 63.11.7. Checked 16.11.2026.

Low-lying places by the Ziemeļupe and Ziemas osta

A very large part of Daugavgrīva lies in the 1% flood-risk zone. In the lowest places, around the Ziemeļupe, Ziemas osta and the nature reserve, groundwater is at a depth of 0–1.5 m, and the ground contains silt and peat. Along Dzintara iela, by contrast, building conditions are favourable, so this is not relevant for every building.

For a ground-floor flat or a basement in a low spot, it matters how the drains behave when the level in the network rises. If the outlet of a floor drain or toilet is lower than the lid of the nearest inspection chamber, the regulations require a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve. In the building's basement that is the building manager's system, in the flat it is yours.

What people usually ask

It is leaking from the flat above, but the neighbour is not home. What should I do in a Daugavgrīva building?

Call the building manager: the manager's person can shut off the riser in the basement, and that will stop the water even if the neighbour's flat is locked. While you wait, collect the water, switch off the electricity in the room and photograph the damage.

I live on the ground floor in a low spot. Do I need a non-return valve?

If any plumbing point is lower than the lid of the nearest sewer inspection chamber, Riga's regulations require a non-return valve or an electrically operated gate valve. On a ground floor above a basement that is usually not the case, in a basement it often is. Describe the situation in the form, and on the call you talk through the job.

Can a heated towel rail be replaced without shutting off the whole stairwell?

If the rail is connected to the hot water riser without a bypass and valves, the riser has to be shut off for the duration of the work, and this is agreed with the building manager. When fitting the new one, a bypass and valves are often added so that next time it can be removed without shutting off the riser.
