More than half of the territory is taken up by pine forest, meadows and drainage ditches, so houses are few and scattered. Here plumbing questions are usually about water in the ground: the basement, the yard sewer line and where the house's drains end up.

The Hapaka grāvis meadows, almost at sea level

The part of the Spilve meadows around the Hapaka grāvis ditch is very low and flat: 0.1–1.0 m above sea level in the middle of the meadows, 1–2 m at the edges. In a wide area there the flood probability reaches 10% or more if the hydraulic structures are not maintained, and 25.3% of the neighbourhood's territory needs engineering preparation before construction.

For a house closer to this part, the direction of the water matters in plumbing. Appliances in a basement that are lower than the lid of the nearest sewer chamber have to be connected with a separate outlet and a valve that stops wastewater from returning. If the house has a holding tank, it has to be sealed and emptied often enough that nothing overflows. With shallow groundwater, the sealing of the tank lid and inlet pipes is as important as the tank itself.

Silt and peat under the sand, dry dunes in the north-west

In most of Kleisti, layers of sand alternate with 3–10 m thick silt and peat, and groundwater is shallower than 1.5 m. In the dune areas in the south-west and north-west, building conditions are favourable; the north-west of the neighbourhood is crossed by the Bolderāja-Priedaine dune ridge, whose highest point reaches 27 m.

So two houses in one neighbourhood can stand on completely different ground. On a dune the yard line usually lies in dry sand, which freezes deeper than clay. In soft ground a pipe can settle and lose its fall, and then the line blocks repeatedly in the same place. If you know what ground your house stands on, note it in the form.

The city network or your own system: the address first

Which streets in Kleisti are on the Rīgas ūdens network cannot be said from a distance. You can check the address on vaitevtovajag.lv or ask Rīgas ūdens. The answer determines which rules apply to your house.

In Riga more than 8,000 residents have still not connected to the central water supply and sewerage. If the street has the network but the house still uses a septic tank, Riga co-financing from €1,000 to €2,000 is available for the connection depending on the length of the service pipe. It is available if at least one person is registered at the property and a contract has been signed with Rīgas ūdens, and it is paid out after construction. The whole process from technical conditions to handover usually takes about 4–6 months, so plan it in good time, not when the septic tank is already overflowing.

  • If the house is connected to the city sewer: the Rīgas ūdens network ends at the outer wall of the inspection chamber, and everything on the house side is the owner's responsibility.
  • If the street has a service pipe up to the plot boundary, connecting is mandatory for a new building that uses water.
  • If the house has a septic tank, Cabinet Regulations No. 384 allow wastewater to be discharged into the environment only through an infiltration system, for example a filtration field.
  • If the water comes from your own borehole, Riga's regulations forbid connecting that network to the city water main.

Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17 (points 3, 5, 63.6 and 63.9) and Cabinet Regulations No. 384 on decentralised sewerage systems. Checked 16.11.2026.

A log building on brick foundations and other old buildings

The Kleisti manor ensemble at Kleistu iela 37, with a manor house and a log building from the end of the 18th century on brick foundations, is a cultural and historical monument of national importance. If plumbing work affects a wooden building, mention it in the form: pipes through a log wall and brick foundations are run differently than in a concrete house, and a heavy water heater is usually not hung on a wooden partition without extra fixings.

What people usually ask

Is there a city sewer in Kleisti?

It depends on the street, and it cannot be said from a distance. Check the address on vaitevtovajag.lv or ask Rīgas ūdens. If the street has no network, the house's wastewater goes into a septic tank or holding tank, and then Cabinet Regulations No. 384 apply to it.

After rain there is water standing in the basement. Can it be pumped into the drains?

Groundwater and rainwater may not be discharged into the city sewer without approval. If the house has a septic tank, excess water overfills it and disrupts treatment. The outlet of a basement pump is usually led away from the house, but not into the drains.

The house stands empty in winter. What should I do with the water system?

Shut off the incoming supply pipe, drain the pipes to a point where they do not freeze, and drain the water heater after disconnecting it from the electricity. If the water comes from a borehole, the manufacturer Grundfos advises draining the pump and the pressure tank too when there is a risk of frost.
