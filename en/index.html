A riser is leaking or the water has already reached the neighbour below

In the flat, first turn off the shut-off valve on your incoming supply pipe, switch off the water heater if it is the hot water that leaks, and warn the neighbour one floor down. If the riser itself leaks, the valve in your flat does not stop it. The riser is the building's common property, and it is shut off by the building manager or a person authorised by them, usually in the basement. The plumber finds the leak and replaces a joint, a seal or a short piece of pipe: fixing a leak is from €40, one floor section of a water riser from €110. More on the pages about an emergency plumber and riser replacement in a flat.

Water does not drain: your branch or the riser of the whole building

If only the sink or the bath is blocked, the cause is usually in the trap or in the horizontal pipe to the riser, and is cleared with a drain snake. If the neighbours' water rises in your bath, the blockage is in the shared riser or in the basement's horizontal drain, and must be reported to the building manager. In a wooden or private house the line runs across the yard to a chamber or to the city network. The Rīgas ūdens network stops at the outer wall of the inspection chamber, and the part of the line past that point is the owner's responsibility. A blockage in the internal drains is from €50, and a yard line is from €50 for the first hour. More: blocked drains.

A water heater in a narrow bathroom and on a lightweight wall

In Soviet-era series blocks the bathroom is small, and the water heater often only fits above the washing machine or in the toilet. A full heater weighs much more than an empty one, so before fitting you need to know whether the wall holds it: a thin partition or the wall of a wooden house needs a different fixing from a brick wall. Fitting or replacing a water heater is from €70, cleaning and descaling from €75, and for a repair the price is set after inspection: whether the heating element, thermostat, anode or safety valve has to be replaced. More: water heater installation and repair.

A radiator and a heated towel rail on the building's heating

In a block of flats a radiator is usually part of the common heating. The Apartment Ownership Law counts heating elements in a flat as common property if their operation depends on the building's utilities, so a radiator replacement is agreed with the building manager, and before the work the riser is shut off and drained. In Soviet-era bathrooms the heated towel rail is often connected to the hot water riser, and it also needs the riser shut off. Replacing a radiator is from €75 and replacing a heated towel rail from €80; shutting off the riser with the building manager is not included in these prices. More: radiators and heating.

A tap, a toilet, a sink and a shower in place of the old bath

Replacing a mixer tap is from €25, fitting a sink from €40, replacing a toilet from €50, connecting a washing machine from €35. For replacing a bath with a shower the price is set after inspection: whether the drain outlet has to be lowered for the slope, whether the water outlets have to be moved, where the riser runs and what the floor level is. Tiling and waterproofing are a different trade, and we do not do them. More: plumbing fitting and replacing a bath with a shower.

In an old wooden house: the incoming supply pipe from the street and pipes by the outer wall

In the wooden buildings of Āgenskalns, Torņakalns and Ķīpsala, pipes can run along an outer wall, under the floor or through several flats, and every house is different. The Rīgas ūdens network ends at the outer wall of the meter chamber, which is 1.5 to 2 m from the street's red line, or at the plot boundary. The incoming supply pipe from there to the house is the owner's responsibility. Replacing water pipes inside the house along a wall or floor is from €10/m, an underground supply pipe is priced after inspection, and building one water or drain outlet is from €35. More: pipe repair and replacement and the article on plumbing in an old wooden house.

Pārdaugava buildings: wooden streets, pre-war buildings and series blocks

The old part of Āgenskalns, with its winding streets between Nometņu iela and Eduarda Smiļģa iela, is protected as an urban planning monument of national importance, and it has many wooden buildings. Most of the buildings appeared after 1812, when the buildings of Āgenskalns were burnt down. Around the market, along Nometņu iela and Slokas iela and in Klīversala the buildings form perimeter blocks as in the centre, while the smaller streets have low-rise blocks of flats with gardens.

Āgenskalna priedes, built in 1958–1962, was the first big Soviet-era residential estate in Riga; the second-phase buildings on Alīses iela are series 464. In Bolderāja, panel buildings began to be built in 1965, Daugavgrīva is dominated by multi-storey blocks of the 1950s and 1960s, and in Dzirciems blocks of flats were built in the 1980s. The northern part of Ķīpsala has kept low-rise buildings from the turn of the 19th and 20th centuries, and the streets of Zasulauks still show wooden houses built in the 1820s.

In Bieriņi, detached-house areas take up 57.5% of the neighbourhood, in Beberbeķi detached houses also dominate, and there the water supply and drains were built in phase 5 of the Cohesion Fund project. In a flat the owner's part is the pipes up to the riser; in a private house it is the whole system up to the boundary of the Rīgas ūdens network. What people usually ask

How quickly do you get in touch after I send a request?

After you submit the form, we contact you within 5 minutes. On the call you agree the time of the visit, because it depends on the job and the place.

Can I send a request at night or at the weekend?

Yes, we also work at night and at weekends. Send the request through the same form, and on the call you agree the time of the visit.

Is it allowed to replace the riser in my flat without the building manager?

The riser is the building's common property. Section 11 of the Apartment Ownership Law provides that work affecting common property needs the consent of the flat owners' association, so ask the building manager first. Once the replacement is agreed, the plumber replaces the section on your floor.

It is leaking at the neighbour below, but my flat is dry. What should I do?

The leak can be in your pipes in the floor or wall, in the riser or in the flat above you. Turn off the valve in your flat and watch whether the leak weakens: if it does, the source is most likely on your side. If not, report it to the building manager and fill in the form.

I have several small jobs: a dripping tap, a leaking toilet, a washing machine to connect. Do I have to send three requests?

No, list everything in one form. Such a visit is usually charged at the hourly rate, which is from €25/h, or by separate price rows. More: plumber for small jobs.

Does the price include materials?

No. The prices on this site are the labour price; materials are extra. If there is a travel charge, it is named on the same call, before the work. All the rows are on the page plumbing prices.

Do you also go to Bolderāja, Bieriņi and Ķīpsala?

Yes. We work in Āgenskalns and in all the Pārdaugava neighbourhoods listed on the page plumber in Pārdaugava, Riga, from Daugavgrīva to Bišumuiža.
