Radiator not heating up - air, valve or riser

A radiator that does not heat up in a flat in Āgenskalns or elsewhere in Pārdaugava, Riga's left bank, usually comes down to one of four causes. The heating has not yet been started in the building, there is air in the radiator, the valve is closed or stuck, or the whole riser is not working. The first three you can check with the palm of your hand in a few minutes. The riser and the building's heating system are the building manager's responsibility.

First: has the heating been switched on in the building

In buildings managed by Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks (Riga's municipal building manager), the heating starts when the air temperature has not risen above +14 °C for at least three days in a row and has dropped to +8 °C at some point. The same weather must also be expected for the next three days. Flat owners can agree to switch the heating on earlier or later. If your neighbours' radiators are cold too, the heating may simply not have started yet.

AS “Rīgas siltums” (Riga's district heating company) transmits and distributes 76% of the heat the city needs. However, switching the heating on in a particular building and the building's internal system are usually handled by the building manager, so start with them if the building is cold.

Heating season start conditions: LSM, 30.09.2025, on Rīgas namu pārvaldnieks buildings; AS “Rīgas siltums” (rs.lv). Checked 16.11.2026.

The palm test: top, bottom, supply pipe

  1. Supply pipe Touch the pipe that leads to the radiator. If it is hot but the radiator is cold, the fault is in the radiator or its valve. If the pipe is cold too, the heat is not reaching your flat.
  2. Top colder than the bottom This is how air in a radiator usually shows itself. It is released by bleeding.
  3. Top warm, bottom cold In this case the cause is usually sludge and deposits at the bottom of the radiator. The manufacturer Worcester Bosch then recommends cleaning the system rather than bleeding it.
  4. Neighbours on the same riser Ask the neighbours above and below whether their radiators on the same riser are warm. If everyone's is cold, the problem is in the riser.

Radiator symptoms and bleeding according to Worcester Bosch guidance (How to bleed a radiator). Checked 16.11.2026.

Air in the radiator: how it is bled

The manufacturer Worcester Bosch describes bleeding like this: first turn the heating off and give the radiators time to cool. Then use a key to open the bleed valve in the radiator's top corner and keep it open until the hissing stops and water comes out without bubbles; then close the valve. Hold a cloth or a small dish under the valve.

In a block of flats you cannot switch the heating off yourself, so open the valve slowly and only a little. If there is no valve, it will not turn, or only water comes out and the radiator is still cold, do not force it. A radiator that runs on the building's heating is common property under Section 4 of the Apartment Ownership Law, and the building manager deals with it from then on.

In a private house, once the air is released there is less water in the system and the pressure drops, so check the heating boiler's pressure gauge afterwards. While the water is cold, the gauge should usually show 1 to 2 bar. According to Vaillant, the boiler shows a warning below 0.8 bar and shuts down below 0.5 bar. If needed, the system is topped up following the boiler's instructions.

Pressure: Vaillant ecoTEC exclusive installation instructions; Worcester Bosch. Values depend on the boiler model. Checked 16.11.2026.

When the whole riser is cold

Air collects at the highest point of the system, so top-floor flats are sometimes cooler. If the whole vertical line is not heating, the cause may be air at the top of the riser, a closed valve in the basement or a fault in the building's system. This cannot be solved from a single flat.

Under Section 6 of the Residential Property Management Law, maintenance and routine repair of the building's services are the building manager's job. Report which flat and floor are cold and whether the neighbours have the same problem. The more precisely you describe the line, the easier it is to find where the water is not flowing.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 4; Residential Property Management Law, Section 6 (likumi.lv). Checked 16.11.2026.

A thermostatic valve stuck after the summer

Inside a thermostatic valve there is a pin that the thermostatic head presses. If the valve was closed over the summer, the pin can stay stuck, and the radiator stays cold even though the head is turned to maximum. With the head removed you can see the pin; if it does not move, the valve has to be repaired or replaced.

To replace a valve in a block of flats, the heating riser has to be shut off and drained, so this job also goes through the building manager. In a private house it is enough to close the radiator valves on both sides, if there are any.

What not to do with a cold radiator

  • Do not unscrew the bleed valve all the way: it can be hard to screw back in under pressure.
  • Do not loosen the radiator nuts or supply connections in a block of flats.
  • Do not turn the riser valves in the basement yourself.
  • Do not bleed a radiator that is warm at the top and cold at the bottom: that usually needs cleaning.
  • In a private house, do not run the circulation pump without water in the system.

The Grundfos ALPHA2 operating instructions forbid running the pump without water and warn that air cannot be released from the system through it. Checked 16.11.2026.

When you need a plumber, and the labour price

A plumber helps if the radiator has no bleed valve, if the air keeps coming back, if the valve is stuck or the radiator leaks. The price of bleeding depends on how many radiators have to be visited, whether a private house's system needs topping up to the right pressure, and whether the air keeps coming back, which means finding where it comes from. A call-out with inspection is from €40, a radiator replacement from €75. This is the labour price; materials are extra.

If there is a travel charge, it is stated on the call before the work. Repair work is described on the page radiator repair, and the building's and private house's heating system on the page heating system repair. All prices: prices.

What people usually ask

Can I bleed a radiator myself in a block of flats?

If the radiator has a bleed valve, you can open it slowly with a key until water comes out. A radiator on the building's heating is common property, so if there is no valve or it will not turn, tell the building manager instead of forcing it.

Why does the boiler show an error after bleeding in a private house?

When air is released, the pressure in the system drops. The manufacturer Vaillant states that below 0.5 bar the boiler shuts down. Top up the system following the boiler's instructions to the cold pressure, usually 1–2 bar.

The heating season has started but the radiators in our building are cold. Who do I tell?

The building manager. They handle switching the heating on and the building's internal systems, and the flat owners may have agreed on a different start time.

The radiator is warm only at the top. What should I do?

If the top is warm and the bottom cold, the cause is usually deposits at the bottom of the radiator. Bleeding does not help then; Worcester Bosch recommends cleaning the system. In a block of flats the building manager deals with it, in a private house it is done by flushing the heating system.
