Water not draining - one sink or the whole soil stack

When water does not drain in a flat in Āgenskalns or elsewhere in Pārdaugava, Riga's left bank, the blockage is usually in one of two places. If water stands in only one sink or bath, the fault is in the trap or in your branch pipe up to the soil stack. If water rises in the bath when the neighbours above use water, the soil stack or the horizontal drain in the basement is blocked, and that is usually the building manager's job.

Four checks before the drain snake and chemicals

  1. Where the water stands Check all the fixtures: kitchen sink, bath, shower, hand basin, toilet. If water stands in only one, the blockage is usually in its trap or in the short pipe behind it.
  2. Whether water appears in another fixture If water bubbles up in the bath when the washing machine empties, both fixtures are on the same branch pipe, and the blockage is further along than their connection.
  3. Whether the level rises by itself If water appears in the bath or shower tray when you are not using anything, it is coming from the upper floors down the soil stack. Then the blockage is below your connection.
  4. Trap over a bucket A kitchen or sink trap can be unscrewed by hand. Put a bucket under it, remove the deposits and screw it back with the same seal.

Your branch pipe and the building's soil stack

Section 3 of the Apartment Ownership Law counts the utility lines up to the common-property risers as part of the flat. So the horizontal pipe from the bath, sink and toilet to the soil stack is usually yours, while the stack itself and the horizontal drain in the basement belong to the whole building. Under Section 6 of the Residential Property Management Law, providing sewerage is the building manager's task.

In a small Soviet-era bathroom the bath, sink and washing machine are often connected to one short pipe that runs to the soil stack in the toilet corner. If the blockage is in this pipe, all three fixtures suffer, but the neighbours do not notice. If the soil stack is blocked on the lower floors, water from above comes out at the lowest free point, for example in a ground-floor bath.

Apartment Ownership Law, Section 3; Residential Property Management Law, Section 6 (likumi.lv). Checked 16.11.2026.

A blocked toilet

If after flushing the water in the toilet rises and drops slowly, the blockage is in the toilet outlet or close to it. Do not flush again and again: each flush adds water and it can overflow the rim. First try a plunger, with enough water to cover its rubber cup, and press evenly.

Rīgas ūdens binding regulations (No. 17, clause 63.11.7) forbid discharging solid objects, textiles, sand, building materials and grease into the drains. So after renovation work, when tools have been washed in the toilet or mortar poured in, the blockage can be solid and a plunger does not help. If the bath also drains slowly along with the toilet, the fault is in the shared pipe further on.

A layer of grease in the kitchen sink pipe

If the kitchen drain is blocked with grease, it is not by chance. According to Rīgas ūdens, oil, grease and coffee grounds stick to the inside of the pipe, and over time a blockage grows from them. So they go in the waste bin, and a strainer for food scraps goes in the sink drain. The layer of grease in the pipe builds up gradually until one day the water no longer drains.

Mechanical cleaning with a rotating drain snake breaks up the grease deposits and frees the pipe. High-pressure jetting removes grease from the pipe walls as well, so it suits a pipe that blocks repeatedly (US Environmental Protection Agency material on sewer cleaning).

SIA “Rīgas ūdens” recommendations (LV portāls, 2026); Riga City Council binding regulations No. 17, clause 63.11.7; US EPA 832-F-99-031. Checked 16.11.2026.

If the pipe was laid with too little fall after renovation

Sometimes water does not drain because of the slope, not a blockage. Without a calculation, Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 sets the fall of internal drains at 30 ‰ (3 cm per metre) for pipes up to 50 mm and at 20 ‰ (2 cm per metre) for pipes of 75–100 mm. If the old cast-iron pipe in a bathroom has been replaced with plastic but the fall is small or the pipe sags in the middle, water stands in it and deposits collect in one place.

Cleaning then helps only for a while, and the pipe has to be re-laid. Read about this on the page waste pipe replacement.

Latvian Building Code LBN 221-15 (likumi.lv), slope clauses 232, 269 and 272. Checked 16.11.2026.

What not to do with a blocked pipe

  • Do not flush the toilet again and again if the water is already rising.
  • Do not pour hot grease down the sink hoping it will run away: it sets further along the pipe.
  • Do not open the soil stack cleanout hatch yourself if the stack is full: water from the upper floors will come out into your flat.
  • Do not push stiff wire through a thin plastic trap, so as not to puncture it.
  • If water is rising from above, do not use your drains and tell the building manager.

When the plumber is the next step, and the price

If the trap is clean and a plunger does not help, the blockage is usually in the pipe behind the wall. What people usually ask

The water drains, but the sink smells of sewage. Why?

The smell is held back by the water in the trap. The UK Building Regulations (Approved Document H) require at least 25 mm of water to remain in the trap. If a sink is not used for a long time, the water evaporates; if the pipe is partly blocked, the trap can be sucked dry when a large amount of water drains away.

Foreign water is rising in the bath on the ground floor. What should I do?

This points to a blockage in the soil stack or the horizontal drain in the basement, below your connection. Stop using water, tell the building manager and take photographs. The horizontal drain and the soil stack are the building's common property.

Will the pipe block again after cleaning?

If the cause was an object, usually not. If it was grease or too little slope, the blockage can come back, so a strainer, putting grease in the waste bin or re-laying the pipe helps.
