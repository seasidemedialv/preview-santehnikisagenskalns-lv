Privacy policy

This page describes what personal data the site santehnikisagenskalns.lv receives through the request form and how it is used. The data is processed by the service provider who receives requests from this site. Updated 16.11.2026.

What data we receive

In the form you enter your name, phone number, neighbourhood, address, type of problem, urgency and a message. The site collects no other personal data.

What the data is needed for

  • To contact you about the request.
  • To understand what job is needed and where it has to be done.
  • To agree the job, the price and the time of the visit.

Where the data goes

After sending, the request goes through the site's form processing into a request system, from which it is received by the specialist who contacts you. We do not sell the data or pass it to others, except where this is needed to provide the service or the law requires it.

How long we keep the data

As long as it is needed to carry out the request and to communicate about it, but no longer than 2 years after the last contact, unless the law requires otherwise.

Cookies

The site does not set cookies.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict its processing and to object to it. Send the request through the same form, stating in the message field what you want. If you believe the data has been processed incorrectly, you can turn to the Data State Inspectorate.
